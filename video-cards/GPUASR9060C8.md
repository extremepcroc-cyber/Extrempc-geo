## Selling Points

The RX 9060 Challenger is ASRock's budget RDNA 4 card, designed for 1080p gaming at 144Hz+. The 8GB GDDR6 buffer is sufficient for most titles at 1080p, and FSR 3 upscaling extends frame rates in supported games. RDNA 4 architecture brings improved ray tracing performance over the previous generation.

The Challenger series is ASRock's entry-level line — below the Steel Legend and Taichi tiers. It uses a dual-fan cooler that's compact enough for most mid-tower cases, and the 550W PSU requirement is lower than competing cards at this price point.

For NZ gamers building a 1080p/144Hz setup, the RX 9060 offers solid performance without the premium of the 9070 or 9070 XT. AMD's FSR 3 upscaling is competitive with NVIDIA's DLSS in supported titles.

## Comparison

vs NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 (GPUASU55D8G) — the RTX 5060 has 8GB GDDR6 and DLSS 4 upscaling. The RX 9060 has FSR 3 and slightly lower power draw. Choose NVIDIA for DLSS 4 and stronger ray tracing; choose AMD for FSR 3 and lower price.

vs AMD Radeon RX 9070 GRE 12GB (GPUASR9070GSLD12) — the 9070 GRE has 12GB VRAM and more CUDA cores. The RX 9060 is for 1080p; the 9070 GRE is for 1440p. Choose the 9060 for budget 1080p; the 9070 GRE for 1440p.

vs Intel Arc B570 (GPUASRB570C) — the B570 has 10GB GDDR6 and XeSS upscaling. The RX 9060 has 8GB and FSR 3. Choose AMD for stronger driver support and FSR 3; choose Intel for the extra 2GB VRAM.

## FAQ

**Q: Is 8GB VRAM enough for 1080p gaming?** — Yes, 8GB is sufficient for most titles at 1080p. Some newer titles at ultra settings may benefit from more VRAM, but 8GB covers the majority of 1080p gaming.

**Q: Does it support FSR 3?** — Yes, the RX 9060 supports FSR 3 with Frame Generation. FSR 3 is available in over 100 supported titles.

**Q: What PSU do I need?** — ASRock recommends a 550W minimum PSU. A quality 600W unit provides headroom for future CPU upgrades.
