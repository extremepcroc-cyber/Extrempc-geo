The Arc B570 is Intel's mid-range Arc card, designed for 1080p/1440p gaming. The 10GB GDDR6 buffer provides headroom for newer titles, and XeSS upscaling extends frame rates in supported games. The Challenger series is ASRock's entry-level line, with a dual-fan cooler and factory overclock.
