## Selling Points

The Arc Pro B65 is Intel's professional-grade Arc card, designed for content creation, AI inference, and professional workloads. The 32GB GDDR6 buffer is substantial for creative applications — enough for large 4K video projects, 3D rendering, and AI model inference.

The blower-style cooler is designed for workstation builds where multiple GPUs may be installed in close proximity. The 4x DisplayPort 2.1 outputs support multi-monitor setups for creative professionals. The 550W PSU requirement is modest for a card with 32GB VRAM.

For NZ content creators and professionals who need a dedicated GPU for creative work, the Arc Pro B65 offers strong performance without the premium of NVIDIA's RTX 4000/5000 Ada series or AMD's Pro Radeon cards. Intel's XeSS is competitive with AMD's FSR 3 and NVIDIA's DLSS in supported applications.
