## FAQ

- **Q: What's the difference between the G502 X PLUS and the G502X?**
  A: Same G502 X generation — optical switches, the control-rich layout, and the optical sensor. The G502X is the wired version; the G502 X PLUS adds wireless. If you never need wireless, the wired G502X is the value pick; the PLUS buys you the wireless freedom on the same platform.

- **Q: Do the optical switches actually fix double-clicking?**
  A: Yes — mechanically it's a different actuation method. Optical switches fire a light beam across a sensor instead of pressing two metal plates together. The double-click failure mode happens when those metal contacts fatigue and bounce; with no physical contact there's nothing to fatigue in that way. It removes the most common left-click death in a mechanical mouse.

- **Q: Why is the G502 so much heavier than a wireless esports mouse?**
  A: Because it's built for a different job — a control-rich, many-button shape for button-heavy MMO, strategy, and productivity work, where you need a larger shell to house the buttons and a stable platform for long, precise sessions. An ultralight wireless mouse drops buttons and mass to optimise for fast-flick FPS. The G502 X line is the right shape if your game is won with hotkeys.

- **Q: What's the warranty like if it develops a fault?**
  A: Logitech's warranty is handled domestically through ExtremePC and Logitech NZ. A switch fault, a scroll-wheel issue, or a connectivity drop is a local RMA — no international shipping, no customs. Keep your ExtremePC proof of purchase for the claim.
