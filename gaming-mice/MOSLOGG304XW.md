## Selling Points

The G304 X keeps the classic G304 shape and takes the weight down to 61 g. The original G304 weighs 99 g and runs on an AA battery, so this is a 38 g reduction on a familiar shape. Lower weight is the usual reason players move to a lighter mouse for flick-heavy shooters such as Valorant and CS2.

The HERO 44K sensor tracks at up to 44,000 DPI, above 678 IPS and above 40 G of acceleration, according to Logitech. Most players run far lower DPI settings (400 to 800 DPI is common in tactical shooters), so the headroom matters less than the sensor's consistency at everyday settings.

There are three ways to connect: the LIGHTSPEED receiver in the box, Bluetooth, or the USB-C cable. One mouse can move between a gaming PC, a laptop and other devices without swapping batteries. Logitech rates battery life at 130+ hours on a full charge, and two minutes of charging gives up to 3.5 hours of use; real-world life varies with how you play.

LIGHTSPEED wireless reports at 1,000 Hz (1 ms) by default. Players who want a higher polling rate can add Logitech's PRO Wireless Receiver (sold separately) to reach up to 8 kHz.

Six programmable buttons and five onboard profiles are set up in Logitech G HUB, which you download from Logitech for advanced features such as DPI, button assignments and lighting.
