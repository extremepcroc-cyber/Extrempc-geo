## FAQ

**Q: Is 49g too light for my grip style?** — 49g is ideal for claw and fingertip grip. If you prefer a heavier mouse for stability, consider the Infinity (65g) or a different model.

**Q: Does 8K polling rate matter for gaming?** — 8K polling (8000Hz) reduces input lag compared to 1000Hz (1000Hz). It's most noticeable in fast-paced FPS games. For MOBA and strategy games, 1000Hz is sufficient.

**Q: What's the difference between 2.4GHz and Bluetooth?** — 2.4GHz offers lower latency and is recommended for gaming. Bluetooth has higher latency and is better for everyday use. The Infinity 97 supports both.
