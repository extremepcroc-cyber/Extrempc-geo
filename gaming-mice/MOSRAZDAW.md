## FAQ

- **Q: Is the Essential's sensor as good as a flagship Razer sensor?**
  A: No — it's Razer's entry-class optical sensor, a tier below the Focus Pro sensor in the DeathAdder V3 / Viper line. At a real CS2/Valorant sensitivity (400–1600 DPI) it won't be the bottleneck for most players, but it's not the top of Razer's sensor stack. If you want the flagship sensor, step up to the V3 line. The exact model and DPI ceiling for this SKU are on the product page.

- **Q: How is the DeathAdder shape compared to a flat ambidextrous mouse?**
  A: The DeathAdder is a purpose-built right-handed ergonomic contour that cradles the rear of the hand — the dominant shape for palm and semi-palm grip. A flat ambidextrous mouse fits "everyone and no one." If you palm-grip, the DeathAdder contour is the difference that makes a lighter or faster mouse actually feel comfortable over a long session.

- **Q: Why no RGB on a Razer mouse?**
  A: The Essential is Razer's entry budget footprint — it drops the Chroma RGB to stay at the value price and keep the shell focused. If you want the Razer ecosystem with Chroma lighting, look at the Basilisk or DeathAdder V3 line. For a fast-flick FPS mouse where you don't need lighting, the Essential is the clean entry pick.

- **Q: What's the warranty like if it develops a fault?**
  A: Razer's global warranty (typically 2 years for gaming peripherals) is handled domestically through ExtremePC and Razer NZ — a local RMA path, not an international shipping process. Keep your ExtremePC proof of purchase for the NZ claim.
