## FAQ

- **Q: Is the G102's sensor as good as a flagship mouse sensor?**
  A: No — it's Logitech's entry-class optical sensor, a tier below the HERO sensor in the G304/G502 line. At a real CS2/Valorant sensitivity (400–1600 DPI) it won't be the bottleneck for most players, but it's not the top of Logitech's sensor stack. If you want the flagship sensor, step up to the G304 or G502 line. The exact model and DPI ceiling for this SKU are on the product page.

- **Q: Does the white finish show up more than a black mouse?**
  A: Yes — a white shell is more visible to scuffs and dust than a black one, so a white mouse needs a slightly more careful desk than a black one. If you want the lowest-maintenance finish, the black G102 is the safer pick. If you want a coordinated light-themed desk, the white G102 is the pick.

- **Q: Why wired when there are cheaper wireless mice?**
  A: Because a wired mouse has zero wireless failure modes — no battery to die, no dongle to lose, no interference. For a first competitive mouse or a budget setup where reliability matters more than the cable, wired is the cleaner, more durable choice. If you want wireless and can add to the budget, the Logitech G304 is the step up.

- **Q: What's the warranty like if it develops a fault?**
