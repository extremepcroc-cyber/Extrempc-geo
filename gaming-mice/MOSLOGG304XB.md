## FAQ

**Q: How long does the battery last?** — Up to 90 hours of continuous gaming. For a typical 3–4 hour gaming session, that's 20+ sessions per charge. The mouse has a USB-C charging port.

**Q: Can I use it with PS5 or Xbox?** — No. The LIGHTSPEED dongle is USB and designed for PC (Windows). For console use, you'd need a wired mouse with a 3.5mm jack.

**Q: Is 63g too light?** — For most gamers, 63g is a sweet spot — light enough to reduce fatigue, heavy enough to feel stable. If you're used to 85g+ mice, you'll need a few days to adjust.

**Q: Does it work with Mac?** — Yes. The LIGHTSPEED dongle is USB and works with Mac. Logitech also offers a wired USB-C connection for charging and use.
