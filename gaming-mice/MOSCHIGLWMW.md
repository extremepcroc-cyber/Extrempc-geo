## FAQ

- **Q: What does 8,000 Hz polling actually do that 1,000 Hz doesn't?**
  A: Polling rate is how often the mouse reports its position. At 1,000 Hz that's every 1 ms; at 8,000 Hz it's every 0.125 ms — eight times more samples per second. In practice the difference is only felt at the top end: sub-millisecond micro-adjustments and very fast target tracking feel marginally tighter. For most players 1,000 Hz is already imperceptible, so 8,000 Hz is a premium spec for players who want the maximum and can tell the difference in fine control, not a universal must-have.

- **Q: What does the on-mouse screen show, and why is that useful?**
  A: It displays live settings on the mouse itself — DPI, battery, and wireless mode — so you can check and change them in a glance without opening a driver. That's genuinely useful for adjusting sensitivity per game or per map, and for a setup where you can't install the driver (a LAN, a tournament PC, a friend's machine).
