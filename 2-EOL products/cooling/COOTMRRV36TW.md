## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between Rainbow Vision and Wonder Vision?** — Both share the same Turbo specs: 3600 RPM pump, 2200 RPM fans, 320W TDP. The difference is in pump head aesthetics — the Rainbow Vision has an RGB ring, the Wonder Vision has a more understated design. The Rainbow Vision is $20 more.

**Q: 320W TDP — is that enough for overclocking?** — 320W is the highest TDP rating in Thermalright's AIO lineup. It covers Ryzen 9 9950X and Core i9-14900K at stock clocks with significant margin, and provides headroom for manual overclocking. For extreme overclocking (liquid nitrogen territory), a custom loop is recommended.

**Q: Does the Turbo mode always run at maximum speed?** — The Turbo designation means the pump and fans are capable of higher RPM than non-Turbo models. Fan curve is PWM-controlled by the motherboard, so idle speeds drop to 500 RPM. The pump maintains higher RPM than non-Turbo models regardless of load.

**Q: Does it support LGA1851?** — Yes, the Rainbow Vision 360 Turbo White includes LGA1851 mounting brackets. Thermalright ships with backplates for all current Intel and AMD sockets.

**Q: How noisy is the Turbo pump?** — The 3600 RPM pump runs at approximately 29 dBA — noticeable in a quiet room but typically masked by fan noise under load. At idle with fans at 500 RPM, the pump is the dominant sound source but remains within acceptable range for most builds.
