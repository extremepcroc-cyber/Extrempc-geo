## FAQ

**Q: Is the white version the same price as black?** — Yes, both the white (COOTMRPV36AW) and black (COOTMRPV36AB) versions are priced at $179. Thermalright does not add a white premium on the Peerless Vision series.

**Q: 270W TDP — is that enough for my CPU?** — 270W covers Ryzen 9 9950X (170W boost) and Core i9-14900K (253W boost) at stock clocks with margin. For aggressive manual overclocking, the Thermalright Rainbow Vision 360 Turbo (320W TDP) provides additional headroom.

**Q: Does it support LGA1851?** — Yes, the Peerless Vision 360 White includes LGA1851 mounting brackets. Thermalright ships with backplates for all current Intel and AMD sockets.

**Q: How long is the warranty?** — Thermalright provides 3-year manufacturer warranty through ExtremePC NZ — the longest standard warranty in the 360mm white AIO segment at this price.

**Q: How does the FDB bearing compare to standard PWM fans?** — FDB (Fluid Dynamic Bearing) fans are rated for 150,000 hours vs ~40,000 hours for sleeve bearings. At 24/7 operation, that's roughly 17 years vs 4.5 years. FDB also runs quieter at higher RPM, which matters during sustained load in NZ summer.
