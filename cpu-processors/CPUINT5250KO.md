## FAQ

- **Q: Does the 250K Plus come with a cooler?**
  A: No. The OEM tray packaging does not include a stock cooler. Pair with a quality tower air (Thermalright Peerless Assassin 120 SE, be quiet! Pure Rock 2 Dark Rock Pro 5) or a 240mm AIO if you want liquid cooling.

- **Q: Does LGA1851 support DDR4?**
  A: No. LGA1851 is DDR5-only. A DDR5 kit is required. If you have an existing DDR4 board, it's LGA1700 and won't fit this CPU.

- **Q: What's the difference between the 250K Plus and the 265K?**
  A: Both are LGA1851 Arrow Lake. The 265K has 12 E-cores vs the 250K Plus's 8, for heavier multi-thread workloads. For gaming the gap is small; for content creation the 265K is the step up.

- **Q: Is the Arc iGPU good enough for gaming?**
  A: For light 1080p gaming and office / media use, yes. For modern AAA at high settings, a discrete GPU is required. The Arc iGPU is the integrated-graphics tier, not a replacement for a discrete card.

- **Q: What cooler do I need for the 250K Plus?**
  A: A quality tower air (Thermalright Peerless Assassin 120 SE, be quiet! Dark Rock Pro 5) or a 240mm AIO. The 125W base envelope is manageable with good airflow.
