## FAQ

- **Q: Does the 14700 come with a cooler?**
  A: No. The OEM tray packaging does not include a stock cooler. Pair with a quality air (Thermalright Peerless Assassin 120 SE, be quiet! Pure Rock 2 Dark Rock Pro 5) or a 240mm/280mm AIO to handle the 219W max turbo envelope.

- **Q: Does the 14700 have integrated graphics?**
  A: No — the "F" variant (no iGPU). A discrete graphics card is required for any display output.

- **Q: Is LGA1700 still worth buying in 2026?**
  A: For a build that already owns a LGA1700 board, yes — the 14700 is the strongest CPU on that socket. For a brand-new build, the AM5 (Ryzen 9000) or LGA1851 (Core Ultra 200S) platforms offer a longer upgrade path.

- **Q: DDR4 or DDR5?**
  A: Board-dependent. LGA1700 boards support either. A DDR5 board (B760 / Z790 DDR5 variant) is the better pairing for the 14700's memory bandwidth needs.

- **Q: 14700 or 14700F — what's the difference?**
  A: Same 20-core silicon. The "F" suffix indicates no integrated graphics (which this SKU already is). The main packaging difference is OEM tray vs retail box. For a build with a discrete GPU and aftermarket cooler, the OEM tray is the value pick.
