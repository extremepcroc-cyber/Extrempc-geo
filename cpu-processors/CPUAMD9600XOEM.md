## FAQ

- **Q: What's the difference between 9600X and 9500F?**
  A: Both are 6-core / 12-thread Zen 5. The 9600X has the higher 5.4 GHz boost clock and includes the Radeon 760M integrated graphics. The 9500F is the no-iGPU variant at a lower price. If a discrete GPU is in the plan and you don't need integrated display, the 9500F is the value pick.

- **Q: Can I use this without a discrete GPU?**
  A: Yes — the 9600X includes the Radeon 760M iGPU for basic display output (office, media, casual gaming at 1080p). It is not a substitute for a discrete GPU in modern AAA gaming.

- **Q: Does it come with a cooler?**
  A: No. The OEM tray packaging does not include a stock cooler. Pair with a quality tower air (Thermalright Peerless Assassin 120 SE, be quiet! Pure Rock 2) or a 120mm/240mm AIO if you want liquid cooling.

- **Q: Is 65W TDP really enough for gaming?**
  A: Yes. The 9600X is in the most efficient tier of the Ryzen 9000 series. A quality air cooler is sufficient; most builds see well under 60°C under gaming load.

- **Q: How long will the AM5 platform be supported?**
  A: AMD has committed to AM5 socket support well past 2027. Future Ryzen 10000/11000-series chips will drop into the same motherboard.
