- **Q: Is 8 cores enough for gaming?**
  A: Yes — 8 cores is the sweet spot for 1440p gaming. Most games in 2026 use 6-8 cores, so the 9700X won't bottleneck the RTX 5070. If you do heavy multitasking or content creation, 8 cores handles those too.
