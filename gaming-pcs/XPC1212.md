## FAQ

- **Q: Is the RTX 5070 Ti's 16GB VRAM enough for 4K?**
  A: For most titles at 4K high/ultra, yes — 16GB of GDDR7 covers it, with DLSS 4 helping in demanding scenes. A few of the most demanding titles at 4K ultra can push past it; if that is your exact use case, the 5080 tier is the safer buy.

- **Q: Is 64GB RAM overkill for gaming?**
  A: For gaming alone, yes — 32GB is plenty for any game in 2026. The 64GB pays off the moment you add productivity: VMs, 4K video, large datasets. If any of those are in your workflow, it's not overkill.

- **Q: How does the 5070 Ti compare to the 5080?**
  A: The 5080 has more CUDA cores and higher bandwidth, delivering noticeably higher 4K framerates and more headroom with ray tracing on. At 1440p the gap is smaller. If 1440p is your main target, the 5070 Ti is the value choice; if 4K is, the 5080 is the performance choice.
