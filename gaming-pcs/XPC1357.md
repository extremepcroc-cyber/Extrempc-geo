## FAQ

- **Q: What does 3D V-Cache actually do for gaming?**
  A: It stacks extra L3 cache on top of the CPU, which keeps more game data close to the cores. In CPU-bound titles (CS2, Valorant, strategy, sim) that translates to measurably higher frame times, especially at 1% lows. In pure GPU-bound AAA it matters less.

- **Q: Is the RX 9070 XT good for 1440p?**
  A: Yes — 16GB of GDDR6 and RDNA 4 make it a strong 1440p card. At high/ultra it handles most current titles comfortably, with FSR 4 pushing supported games well past 120 FPS. Ray tracing is playable at 1440p.

- **Q: Why an all-AMD build rather than AMD CPU + NVIDIA GPU?**
