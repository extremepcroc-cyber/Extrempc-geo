## FAQ

- **Q: Is the 1000W PSU overkill for the RTX 5080?**
  A: Yes — the RTX 5080 runs fine on 850W. The 1000W is for future-proofing. If you plan to upgrade to an RTX 5090 later, the 1000W PSU handles it without replacement.

- **Q: Is 1TB SSD enough for 4K games?**
  A: 4K games are larger — Call of Duty and Starfield can be 150-200GB each. 1TB handles Windows + 5-8 large games. Consider adding a second NVMe drive for a large library. The B850M has extra M.2 slots.

- **Q: Is 32GB RAM enough with the 9900X?**
