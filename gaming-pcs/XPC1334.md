## FAQ

- **Q: Is the RX 9070 XT good for 1440p?**
  A: Yes — 16GB of GDDR6 and RDNA 4 make it a strong 1440p card. At high/ultra it handles most current titles comfortably, and FSR 4 frame generation pushes supported games well past 120 FPS. Ray tracing is playable at 1440p.

- **Q: Why Windows 11 Pro and not Home?**
  A: This build ships with Pro, which adds BitLocker encryption, Hyper-V, and Group Policy. If you run a VM, dev container, or business data on the machine, those features are worth the included license.

- **Q: What do I give up choosing AMD over NVIDIA?**
  A: Mainly DLSS (NVIDIA's frame generation and upscaling, considered the benchmark) and NVENC (NVIDIA's hardware video encoder). AMD's equivalents are FSR 4 and the AMF encoder. If you rely heavily on either, the NVIDIA builds are the safer fit; otherwise the AMD stack is the value pick.
