## FAQ

- **Q: Is the RTX 5080 good for 4K gaming?**
  A: Yes — the RTX 5080 is NVIDIA's 4K entry GPU. At 4K high settings, it handles most AAA titles with 60-100+ FPS. DLSS 4 Quality mode pushes supported titles higher. For 4K ultra with full ray tracing, you'd want the RTX 5090.

- **Q: Is 8 cores enough to feed the RTX 5080?**
  A: At 1440p and especially 4K, the GPU is the bottleneck, not the CPU. The 9700X's 8 cores are more than enough. At 1080p competitive settings, you'd benefit more from a faster CPU, but that's not this build's target.

- **Q: Is 1TB SSD enough for 4K games?**
  A: 4K games are larger — Call of Duty and Starfield can be 150-200GB each. 1TB handles Windows + 5-8 large games. Consider adding a second NVMe drive for a large library. The B850M has extra M.2 slots.

- **Q: Does the 360mm AIO fit the case?**
  A: Yes — the Segotep Endura Pro+ supports 360mm radiators. The AIO is pre-installed and tested before shipping.

- **Q: Can I upgrade to an RTX 5090 later?**
  A: The 850W PSU and case can handle an RTX 5090 upgrade. Check the GPU's physical length against the case's GPU clearance before purchasing.
