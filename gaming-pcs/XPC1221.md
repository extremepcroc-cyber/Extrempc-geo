## FAQ

- **Q: Is the RTX 5070 Ti's 16GB VRAM enough for 4K?**
  A: For most titles at 4K high/ultra, yes — 16GB of GDDR7 covers it, with DLSS 4 helping in demanding scenes. A few of the most demanding titles at 4K ultra can push past it; for that exact use case the 5080 tier is safer.

- **Q: Why an 8-core CPU with a 16GB GPU instead of more cores?**
  A: Deliberate split. 1440p/4K AAA is GPU-bound, so the 5070 Ti does the heavy lifting; the 9700X's 16 threads cover streaming and multitasking. If your workload is mostly CPU-bound (compiling, rendering), the 12-core 9900X builds are the better fit.

- **Q: Is 64GB RAM overkill for gaming?**
  A: For gaming alone, yes — 32GB is plenty for any game in 2026. The 64GB pays off the moment you add VMs, 4K video, or large datasets to the same machine.
