## FAQ

**Q: Does the Nexus PX Black support a 280mm AIO?**
Front 240mm support is confirmed; 280mm depends on the specific mount geometry. Check the product page for exact radiator slot dimensions before purchasing a 280mm AIO.

**Q: Is the tempered glass tinted or clear?**
Standard budget cases use lightly tinted or clear TG. The Nexus PX Black typically uses a light tint or untinted panel — components inside appear in their natural colours rather than a darkened view.

**Q: Will an RTX 4070 Super fit?**
RTX 4070 Super Founders Edition is 267mm — well within 340mm clearance. Most AIB RTX 4070 Super variants run 280–320mm, also within spec. Check your specific AIB card dimensions to confirm.

**Q: Does it include case fans?**
