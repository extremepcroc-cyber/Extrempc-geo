## FAQ

**Q: Does the Prime D include any case fans?**
Typically no fans included at this price point — the Prime D ships as a bare chassis. Plan for at least one 120mm rear exhaust fan.

**Q: Will an ATX motherboard fit?**
No — the Prime D supports Micro-ATX (244 × 244mm) and smaller. Full ATX (305 × 244mm) will not fit. Check your motherboard form factor before purchasing.

**Q: Is there a tempered glass version of the Prime D?**
