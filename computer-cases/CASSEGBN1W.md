## FAQ

**Q: Is the Nexus 1 the same as the Nexus PX?**
No — Nexus 1 and Nexus PX are different models. Nexus 1 is the entry model in the Nexus line.

**Q: Will a 160mm DeepCool AK400 fit?**
Yes — the AK400 is exactly 160mm, which matches the Nexus 1's maximum clearance. It fits but without any additional margin.

**Q: Does it support a 240mm AIO?**
Check the product page for confirmed radiator positions. The Nexus 1 may support a 240mm AIO in the front or top position, but the Endura 240S (same price) is explicitly designed and named for this use case — safer choice for AIO builds.

**Q: Are there case fans included?**
Typically no fans at this price tier. Budget for 1× 120mm rear exhaust as a minimum; add front fans for improved airflow.
