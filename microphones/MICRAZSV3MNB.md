## Selling Points

The Seiren V3 Mini is Razer's most compact USB microphone, designed for streamers and content creators who want broadcast-quality audio without the bulk of a full-size condenser mic. The 14mm condenser capsule captures 24-bit/96kHz audio, and the built-in shock mount and pop filter mean no extra accessories needed.

The ultra-compact foldable stand takes up minimal desk space — useful for NZ gamers running tight desk setups in bedrooms or small apartments.
