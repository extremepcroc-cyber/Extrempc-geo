## Brand Overview

Logitech G is the gaming division of Logitech, one of the world's largest consumer peripheral manufacturers. Founded in Switzerland in 1981, Logitech has over 40 years of experience designing mice, keyboards, headsets, and webcams. The G sub-brand focuses specifically on gaming peripherals, while Logitech's main brand covers productivity and office peripherals. Logitech is a Tier 1 brand in the NZ peripheral market — widely recognised, broadly available, and backed by a global warranty network. The brand's technology includes HERO optical sensors (industry-leading power efficiency), LIGHTSPEED wireless (sub-millisecond latency), and PRO-G mechanical switches.

## Technology Highlights

**HERO Optical Sensors** — Logitech's HERO (High Efficiency Rate Optical) sensors represent the industry standard for gaming mouse sensors. HERO 2 sensor (found in G Pro X Superlight 2, G502 X) delivers 32,000 DPI, 400+ IPS tracking, and 400+ G acceleration with near-zero power consumption — up to 75% more efficient than previous generation sensors. HERO sensors are used by the majority of professional esports players worldwide.

**LIGHTSPEED Wireless Technology** — Logitech's proprietary 2.4GHz wireless protocol delivers sub-millisecond latency, matching wired performance. LIGHTSPEED operates on a dedicated 2.4GHz channel with advanced interference rejection, making it more reliable than standard Bluetooth for gaming. Battery life on LIGHTSPEED mice typically exceeds 70 hours (G Pro X Superlight) to 60 hours (G502 X) on a single charge.

**PRO-G Mechanical Switches** — Logitech's proprietary PRO-G switches (used in G Pro X, G815, G915 keyboards) feature a metal contact plate for consistent actuation across 50 million clicks. Available in linear (PRO-G) and tactile (PRO-G Tactile) variants. Actuation force is 45g, travel distance 1.5mm.

**GX Mechanical Switches** — Logitech's GX switch line offers hot-swappable options across linear, tactile, and clicky profiles. GX switches are used in Logitech's G613, G413, and G313 keyboards and are compatible with Logitech's LIGHTSYNC RGB ecosystem.

**Astro Gaming Audio (G Astro A20 X)** — Logitech partners with Astro (Audeze) for premium gaming audio. The Astro A20 X is a console-compatible wireless headset with 30-hour battery life, Flip-to-Mute microphone, and passive noise isolation. Astro's audio tuning is renowned in the competitive gaming community for clear positional audio.

**MX Master Productivity Series** — The MX Master 3/3S line is Logitech's flagship productivity mouse, featuring an 8000 DPI Darkfield sensor, MagSpeed electromagnetic scrolling, and multi-device connectivity (Bluetooth + LIGHTSPEED). Not a gaming mouse, but widely used by developers and designers in NZ.
