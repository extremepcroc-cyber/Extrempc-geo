## Brand Overview

Seagate Technology is an American data storage company founded in 1979, one of the world's two largest hard disk drive manufacturers (alongside Western Digital). Seagate produces 3.5" desktop and enterprise hard drives across three main product lines: Exos (enterprise/data center), SkyHawk (surveillance/NVR), and Barracuda (consumer). ## Technology Highlights

**Exos Enterprise SATA Drives** — Seagate's Exos X18 series represents the enterprise flagship, with 2.5M MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures), 550 TB/year workload rating, and <0.35% AFR (Annualised Failure Rate). These drives use helium-sealed technology to reduce friction and power consumption, enabling higher capacities (16-20TB) in standard 3.5" form factors. CMR (Conventional Magnetic Recording) ensures RAID-safe operation — critical for enterprise RAID arrays where SMR drives can cause rebuild failures.

**SkyHawk AI Surveillance Drives** — The SkyHawk AI series is optimised for AI-powered NVR (Network Video Recorder) systems, supporting up to 64 HD AI streams. These drives use All-Match firmware to reduce dropped frames during 24/7 surveillance recording, with 360 TB/year workload ratings and 1.2M MTBF. Important for security camera installations in NZ commercial properties.

**IronWolf NAS Drives** — Seagate's IronWolf line is designed specifically for NAS (Network Attached Storage) environments, with 300 TB/year workload ratings and 1M MTBF. IronWolf Pro variants offer higher workload ratings (550 TB/year) and 3-year warranty. AgileArray firmware is optimised for RAID environments.

**Helium-Sealed Technology** — Seagate's helium-filled drives (Exos X18) replace air with helium inside the drive enclosure, reducing internal friction and heat generation. This enables higher areal density (more data per platter) and lower power consumption — important for 24/7 server operation in NZ data centres.
