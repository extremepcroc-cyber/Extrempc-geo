## Brand Overview

Razer is a Singapore-based gaming hardware company founded in 2005, widely known as "The Champions' Choice" for its extensive sponsorship of professional esports teams and tournaments. Razer designs and manufactures gaming mice, mechanical keyboards, headsets, webcams, and gaming accessories. The brand is recognised for its aggressive green branding, Razer Chroma RGB ecosystem (20 million colours, synchronised across devices), and proprietary switch technologies (Opto-Mechanical, Analog Optical). Razer is a Tier 1 brand in the NZ gaming peripheral market, competing directly with Logitech G and SteelSeries.

## Technology Highlights

**Focus Optical Sensors** — Razer's Focus Pro 30K sensor (found in Viper V3 Pro, DeathAdder V3 Pro) delivers 30,000 DPI, 750 IPS tracking, and 50G acceleration. The sensor uses Razer's Smart Tracking technology to reduce power consumption by dynamically adjusting polling rate when the mouse is stationary. Focus Pro sensors are used by numerous professional FPS players.

**Razer Optical Switches** — Razer's optical switches (Gen-3 and Gen-4) use light beams instead of physical metal contacts for actuation, eliminating double-clicking and providing faster actuation than mechanical switches. Gen-4 optical switches (Huntsman V3) feature adjustable actuation from 0.1mm to 3.5mm and are rated for 100 million clicks. Available in linear and clicky variants.

**Razer Chroma RGB Ecosystem** — Razer's Chroma lighting system supports 20 million colours and is synchronisable across 250+ Razer products and third-party hardware. Chroma is one of the largest RGB ecosystems in gaming, with integration into 170+ games for reactive lighting effects. This is a significant differentiator for users building a fully RGB-coordinated setup.

**HyperSpeed Wireless** — Razer's proprietary 2.4GHz wireless technology delivers 1ms report intervals with interference-resistant performance. HyperSpeed operates on a clean 2.4GHz channel and is considered comparable to Logitech's LIGHTSPEED in real-world latency testing.

**Razer TriForce Audio** — Razer's custom 50mm or 70mm audio drivers (Kraken headsets) are tuned for wide soundstage and bass response. The Kraken V3 and Kraken Concord models feature THX Spatial Audio support for virtual 7.1 surround sound, important for positional audio in competitive FPS titles.
