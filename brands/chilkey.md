## Brand Overview

Chilkey is a Chinese mechanical keyboard and gaming peripheral brand targeting the enthusiast keyboard market at entry-to-value-tier pricing. The brand is known for tri-mode wireless keyboards with hot-swappable PCBs, Hall-effect magnetic switches, and distinctive colourway designs. Chilkey also produces gaming mice (including 8K polling rate models with OLED screens) and keyboard accessories like desk mats. The brand appeals to budget-conscious keyboard enthusiasts who want modern features (magnetic switches, wireless, hot-swap) without the premium pricing of established brands.

## Technology Highlights

**Tri-Mode Wireless Keyboards** — The ND75 supports wired USB-C, 2.4GHz wireless, and Bluetooth connectivity. Hot-swappable PCBs allow switch replacement without soldering. Available with Dopamine Blue switches (tactile) at value-tier pricing.

**Hall-Effect Magnetic Switches** — The Slice68 HE uses WS Flux magnetic switches with rapid trigger support, providing adjustable actuation points for competitive gaming. This places Chilkey in the magnetic switch segment alongside Epomaker and MCHOSE at this price tier.

**8K Wireless Gaming Mice with OLED Screens** — The Glacier LW 8K series supports 8000Hz polling rate wireless connectivity with an integrated OLED screen displaying real-time stats (DPI, polling rate, battery). This is a feature combination typically found on premium-tier mice.
