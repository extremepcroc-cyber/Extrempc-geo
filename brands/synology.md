## Brand Overview

Synology is a Taiwanese NAS (Network Attached Storage) manufacturer founded in 2000, best known for its DiskStation NAS appliances and DiskStation Manager (DSM) operating system. Synology also produces its own line of NAS-optimised hard drives (HAT3300 and HAT3310 series) specifically designed for use in Synology NAS systems. These drives offer extended warranty benefits when used in compatible Synology NAS units — up to 5 years compared to the standard 3-year warranty. In New Zealand, Synology NAS drives are stocked through ExtremePC.

## Technology Highlights

**NAS-Optimised Firmware** — Synology's HAT series drives use firmware specifically tuned for NAS environments, with optimised error recovery control (ERC/TLER) to prevent RAID array dropouts during drive errors. This is critical for NAS RAID stability — consumer drives often abort read operations too quickly, causing RAID controllers to mark the drive as failed.

**Extended Warranty with Synology NAS** — Synology NAS drives come with a 3-year standard warranty that extends to 5 years when used in compatible Synology NAS systems. This is a unique benefit that incentivises users to pair Synology drives with Synology NAS hardware, creating a fully supported ecosystem.

**CMR Recording** — All Synology NAS drives use CMR (Conventional Magnetic Recording), ensuring RAID-safe operation. SMR (Shingled Magnetic Recording) drives are unsuitable for RAID environments due to slow write speeds during rebuilds.

**Vibration Resistance** — Synology NAS drives include RV (Rotational Vibration) sensors to handle the multi-bay vibration environment of NAS enclosures. This is important for 4-bay, 8-bay, and 12-bay NAS units where drive vibration can impact long-term reliability.
