## Brand Overview

Thermalright is a Taiwanese cooling manufacturer founded in 2006, widely regarded in the PC enthusiast community as offering the best price-to-performance ratio in CPU air cooling. The brand is known for tower coolers that match or exceed the cooling performance of Corsair, NZXT, and be quiet! equivalents at 30-50% lower NZD pricing. Thermalright's Peerless Assassin and Phantom Spirit series are consistently top-rated in independent cooling benchmarks. In New Zealand, Thermalright coolers are stocked through ExtremePC.

## Technology Highlights

**Dual-Tower Air Coolers** — Thermalright's Peerless Assassin 120 and Phantom Spirit 120 use dual-tower heat pipe designs with 6-7 copper heat pipes and dual 120mm fans. These coolers handle CPUs up to 250W TDP, matching the cooling performance of $150+ NZD coolers from Corsair and be quiet! at value-tier pricing. The Phantom Spirit 120 features FDB (Fluid Dynamic Bearing) fans rated for 150,000 hours.

**FDB Fan Technology** — Thermalright's FDB fans use fluid dynamic bearings for smooth operation across the entire speed range, with near-zero wear over the fan's lifespan. FDB fans are quieter than PWM-controlled sleeve bearings and more durable than standard hydraulic bearings — important for 24/7 operation in NZ summer conditions.

**AIO Liquid Coolers** — Thermalright's Frozen Notte and Phantom Spirit AIO series offer 240mm and 360mm radiator options at prices below Corsair H100i and NZXT Kraken equivalents. These AIOs use ARGB fans and addressable pump heads for aesthetic builds.

**Silver Arrow Single-Tower Coolers** — The Silver Arrow series provides single-tower cooling for budget builds and compact cases where dual-tower coolers don't fit. Still competitive in cooling performance per dollar.
