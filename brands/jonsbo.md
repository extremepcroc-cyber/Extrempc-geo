## Brand Overview

Jonsbo is a Chinese computer case manufacturer founded in 2006, specialising in compact and mini-ITX form factor cases. The brand is known for innovative space-efficient designs that fit powerful components into small footprints, including open-frame cases, cube cases, and compact tower designs. Jonsbo cases are popular among HTPC (Home Theatre PC) builders, small-form-factor enthusiasts, and users who prioritise desk space. In New Zealand, Jonsbo cases are stocked through ExtremePC.

## Technology Highlights

**Compact Form Factor Expertise** — Jonsbo's N-series (N2, N3, N6) cases are designed for mini-ITX and M-ATX motherboards, fitting powerful builds into small footprints. The N2 supports SFX power supplies and low-profile CPU coolers, while the N3 and N6 offer more clearance for mid-tower coolers and longer GPUs. These cases are ideal for NZ users with limited desk space.

**Open-Frame Designs** — Jonsbo's open-frame cases (U series) provide maximum airflow and easy build access at the cost of dust protection. These are suitable for test benches, IT labs, and users who prioritise cooling over aesthetics.

**Tempered Glass & Aluminium Construction** — Higher-end Jonsbo cases feature tempered glass side panels and aluminium frames for premium aesthetics at value-tier pricing. The N6 series uses a combination of aluminium and tempered glass for a clean, modern look.

**Modular Drive Bays** — Jonsbo cases feature modular SSD/HDD mounting options, allowing users to configure storage layouts based on their needs. This is important for NAS builds and multi-drive setups.
