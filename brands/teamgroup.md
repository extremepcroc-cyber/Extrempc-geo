## Brand Overview

TeamGroup is a Taiwanese memory and storage manufacturer founded in 2004, producing DDR4/DDR5 RAM, NVMe/SATA SSDs, and USB flash drives. The brand is known for competitive pricing in the memory segment, particularly in the DDR5 enthusiast market with RGB-lit kits. TeamGroup's T-Force gaming sub-brand targets PC builders who want performance memory at value-tier NZD pricing. In New Zealand, TeamGroup RAM and SSDs are stocked through ExtremePC.

## Technology Highlights

**DDR5 Memory Kits** — TeamGroup's T-Force Delta and Vulcan DDR5 kits support speeds from 4800MHz to 7200MHz+, targeting Intel and AMD enthusiast platforms. RGB-lit variants (Delta RGB) feature addressable LED strips for synchronisation with motherboard RGB ecosystems. Priced below Corsair Vengeance and G.Skill Trident equivalents.

**NVMe SSDs** — TeamGroup's MP44L and MP33L NVMe drives use PCIe 4.0 and PCIe 3.0 interfaces respectively, with read speeds up to 7,000 MB/s (MP44L). These drives use DRAM-less designs with HMB (Host Memory Buffer) technology, suitable for budget gaming and office builds.

**LPDDR Support** — TeamGroup manufactures LPDDR4/LPDDR5 modules for laptop and mini-PC upgrades, a segment many mainstream memory brands don't cover.
