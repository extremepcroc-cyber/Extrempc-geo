## Brand Overview

AOC (Art of Creation) is a global monitor manufacturer owned by TPV Technology, one of the world's largest display panel makers. Founded in 1935, AOC has over 80 years of display manufacturing experience and is one of the top-selling monitor brands globally. The brand covers gaming monitors (Fast IPS panels up to 420Hz, curved VA panels), business monitors (USB-C docking, 4K IPS), and budget displays. In New Zealand, AOC is positioned as a value-to-value-premium monitor brand, offering competitive specs at prices below Samsung, LG, and ASUS.

## Technology Highlights

**Fast IPS Panels (Gaming Line)** — AOC's gaming monitors use Fast IPS panels with response times as low as 0.3ms (GtG). The 25G4K reaches 420Hz refresh rate — one of the highest refresh rates available at AOC's price tier — making it suitable for competitive FPS titles where frame rate directly impacts responsiveness. The 27G50Z (240Hz) and C27G4Z (280Hz curved) sit in the high-refresh gaming segment.

**Curved VA Panels** — The CQ32G4 uses a 1500R curved VA panel with 180Hz refresh rate and 1ms response time. VA panels offer higher contrast ratios (3000:1 native) than IPS, providing deeper blacks — important for gaming immersion and dark-room use. The 32" size with QHD resolution is a popular combination for immersive gaming.

**USB-C Docking (Business Line)** — The Q27P3CV and U27B3CF business monitors feature USB-C connectivity with power delivery (up to 90W), allowing a single cable to connect a laptop for display, charging, and data. This is a significant convenience for office workers and remote workers who want a clean desk setup.

**4K UHD Displays** — AOC's 4K monitors (U27B35, U27B3CF, U27U3XD) use IPS panels with 4ms response times, suitable for productivity, content creation, and design work. The U27B3CF includes USB-C docking for laptop connectivity.
