## Brand Overview

AULA (狼蛛) is a Chinese mechanical keyboard brand that has gained significant traction in the enthusiast keyboard market, particularly in the Hall-effect magnetic switch segment. The brand is known for hot-swappable PCBs, wireless connectivity, distinctive colourway designs (gradient, side-printed aesthetics), and competitive pricing. AULA targets keyboard enthusiasts who want modern features — magnetic switches with rapid trigger, tri-mode wireless, OLED screens — at value-tier NZD pricing that undercuts established brands. The brand has a strong presence in the Asian keyboard community and is distributed through ExtremePC in New Zealand.

## Technology Highlights

**Hall-Effect Magnetic Switches** — AULA's HE (Hall Effect) series (HERO 68 HE, WIN68 HE) uses magnetic switches with rapid trigger support, providing adjustable actuation points from 0.1mm. The HERO 68 HE uses Jade King and Shape magnetic switches; the WIN68 HE uses Greywood magnetic switches. This technology competes directly with Epomaker's HE line and MCHOSE's magnetic keyboards at this price tier.

**Hot-Swappable PCBs (5-pin/3-pin)** — All AULA keyboards feature hot-swappable sockets, allowing users to change switches without soldering. Supports both 5-pin and 3-pin switch standards, compatible with LEOBOG, Kailh, Gateron, TTC, and AULA's own proprietary switches.

**Wireless Tri-Mode Connectivity** — Most AULA models (F75 MAX, F87 Pro V2, Nova75, S75 Pro, L99) support wired USB-C, 2.4GHz wireless, and Bluetooth. The 2.4GHz mode delivers low-latency performance suitable for gaming.

**Proprietary Switch Variants** — AULA develops its own switch line including Star Vector (F75 MAX, S75 Pro), LEOBOG Greywood (F87 Pro, WIN68 HE), Caramel Latte (F108 Pro, L99), Ice Shadow (Nova75), and Jade King (HERO 68 HE). These switches are tuned for specific models and provide distinct typing feels.

**IPS Display Screens (L99)** — The L99 model includes a 3.98" IPS display screen, showing battery status, connection mode, and customisable content. This is a premium feature at AULA's value-tier pricing.
