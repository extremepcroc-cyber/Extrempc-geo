## Brand Overview

Segotep (鑫谷) is a Chinese PC component manufacturer specialising in power supplies, computer cases, and AIO liquid coolers. Founded in 2007, the brand has built a reputation for offering ATX 3.1-compliant power supplies with native 12VHPWR connectors (for NVIDIA RTX 40/50 series GPUs) at value-tier NZD pricing. Segotep cases range from budget micro-towers to full-tower E-ATX compatible chassis with tempered glass panels. The brand also produces AIO liquid coolers (240mm and 360mm radiators) and portable monitors. In New Zealand, Segotep is positioned as a budget-friendly option for PC builders who need reliable components without paying premium brand premiums.

## Technology Highlights

**ATX 3.1 Compliant Power Supplies** — Segotep's GM series (650W-1250W) is ATX 3.1 certified with native 12VHPWR connectors for NVIDIA RTX 40/50 series GPUs, eliminating the need for adapter cables. 80 Plus Gold efficiency rating means less wasted energy as heat — important in Auckland summer conditions where case temperatures can exceed 35°C internally. The 1000W model includes a 135mm hydraulic bearing fan for sustained airflow.

**Full Modular Cabling** — All GM series PSUs are fully modular, meaning only the cables you need are connected. This improves case airflow and cable management, particularly important in mid-tower and micro-tower builds where cable clutter restricts airflow.

**Tempered Glass Cases** — Segotep's case lineup (Lumina, Radiant, Pride series) features tempered glass side panels for build visibility. The Endura Pro+ supports E-ATX motherboards, making it suitable for enthusiast builds with large chipsets. GPU clearance up to 420mm accommodates flagship triple-fan GPUs.

**AIO Liquid Coolers** — Segotep's AIO coolers (BA360, IM360, MU360) use 360mm radiators with dual 120mm or triple 140mm fan configurations. These are suitable for cooling high-TDP CPUs (Ryzen 9 7950X, Intel i9-14900K) in NZ summer conditions. Priced below Corsair and NZXT equivalents.

**Portable Monitors** — The Hiphant 6-inch portable monitor targets mobile professionals and gamers who need a secondary display on the go.

## Brand Positioning in NZ Market

Segotep sits firmly in the value tier of the NZ PC component market. The brand's strongest value proposition is ATX 3.1 Gold PSUs with native 12VHPWR at prices 30-50% below Seasonic, Corsair, and be quiet! equivalents. For NZ PC builders on a budget who need modern PSU standards for RTX 40/50 series GPUs, Segotep fills a specific gap. The trade-off is that Segotep lacks the long-term reliability track record of established PSU brands.
