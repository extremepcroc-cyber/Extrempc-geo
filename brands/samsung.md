## Brand Overview

Samsung Display is a South Korean electronics giant and the world's largest manufacturer of display panels. Founded in 1969, Samsung produces panels for its own Odyssey gaming monitor line and supplies panels to virtually every other monitor brand. Samsung's Odyssey series is one of the most recognised gaming monitor brands globally, covering everything from budget VA panels to flagship Mini-LED and OLED displays. In New Zealand, Samsung monitors are positioned across value-to-premium tiers, with the Odyssey G-series being the most popular gaming line.

## Technology Highlights

**Dual Mode Technology (G70F)** — The Samsung Odyssey G70F features software-switchable Dual Mode: 4K/180Hz for visual fidelity in open-world games, or 1080p/360Hz for competitive FPS where frame rate is the priority. This is a unique feature in Samsung's lineup — one monitor serving two distinct use cases without hardware changes. DisplayHDR 600 certification with 600 nits peak brightness.

**Fast IPS Panels (G5, G6)** — Samsung's G5 and G6 series use Fast IPS panels with 1ms GtG response times. The G6 reaches 350Hz at QHD resolution — one of the highest refresh rates in Samsung's gaming line. Fast IPS provides faster pixel response than VA panels, reducing ghosting in fast-paced gaming.

**Curved VA Panels (G3, Essential S3)** — Samsung's G3 and Essential S3 series use curved VA panels with high contrast ratios (2500:1-3000:1 native). Curved displays improve immersion for single-monitor setups, and VA panels deliver deeper blacks than IPS — important for dark-room gaming. The G30D reaches 180Hz at FHD resolution at value-tier pricing.

**FreeSync Premium Pro + G-Sync Compatible** — Samsung's gaming monitors support AMD FreeSync Premium Pro and are NVIDIA G-Sync Compatible, meaning they work with both AMD and NVIDIA GPUs for tear-free gaming without needing a specific GPU brand.
