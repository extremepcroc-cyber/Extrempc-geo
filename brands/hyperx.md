## Brand Overview

HyperX is a gaming peripheral brand owned by HP Inc., acquired in 2021 for approximately $350 million. Originally founded in 2002 as a memory module manufacturer (HyperX DDR RAM), the brand pivoted to gaming peripherals in 2013 with the launch of the Cloud headset line. HyperX is now one of the most recognised gaming headset brands globally, with the Cloud series consistently ranked among the best gaming headsets in its price tier. The brand is known for comfort-focused design, durable build quality, and clear audio tuning optimised for competitive gaming communication. In New Zealand, HyperX headsets are stocked through ExtremePC.

## Technology Highlights

**Cloud Series Audio Drivers** — HyperX's signature headsets use custom-tuned 53mm drivers (Cloud III) or 40mm drivers (Stinger 2 series) with a focus on clear vocal reproduction and positional audio. The Cloud III uses a redesigned headband with memory foam ear cushions rated for extended comfort during 6+ hour sessions. Audio tuning prioritises voice clarity for team communication over bass-heavy gaming immersion.

**DTS Headphone:X Spatial Audio** — The Cloud Stinger 2 series supports DTS Headphone:X virtual surround sound, delivering multi-channel spatial audio through stereo headphones. This is important for competitive FPS titles (Valorant, CS2, Apex Legends) where directional audio cues matter. DTS Headphone:X is also available on the Cloud Stinger 2 Core variant.

**Wireless 2.4GHz Connectivity** — HyperX's wireless headsets (Cloud III S Wireless, Cloud Jet Wireless, Cloud Stinger 2 Wireless, Cloud Stinger Core Wireless) use 2.4GHz dedicated wireless with low-latency audio transmission. Battery life ranges from 25 hours (Cloud Jet Wireless) to 45+ hours (Cloud Stinger 2 Wireless) depending on model and RGB usage.

**Pulsefire Haste 2 Gaming Mice** — The Pulsefire Haste 2 Core and Mini variants are lightweight wireless gaming mice. The Mini weighs approximately 51g, placing it in the sub-60g competitive segment. Features HyperX's wireless technology with multi-device support.

**Comfort-First Design Philosophy** — HyperX headsets are consistently rated for comfort in independent reviews. The Cloud III uses a steel slider frame with memory foam ear cushions and a breathable leatherette cover. The Cloud Mini is specifically designed for smaller head sizes, a niche most gaming headset brands ignore.
