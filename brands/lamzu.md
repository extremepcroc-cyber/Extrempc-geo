## Brand Overview

Lamzu is a Chinese gaming peripheral brand that has rapidly gained traction in the competitive esports scene, particularly among FPS players and high-level tournament competitors. The brand is known for ultra-lightweight wireless gaming mice under 50g, 8000Hz polling rate technology, and premium optical switches. Lamzu's product philosophy targets serious gamers who prioritise raw performance metrics — weight, polling rate, sensor precision — over RGB aesthetics or brand heritage. The brand has built a dedicated following in the Asian esports community and is expanding into Western markets through retailers like ExtremePC in New Zealand.

## Technology Highlights

**8000Hz Polling Rate** — Lamzu's flagship wireless mice (Maya X, Thorn V2 8K, PARO) support 8000Hz polling via a dedicated USB dongle, delivering 0.125ms report intervals versus the standard 1000Hz (1ms) found on most gaming mice. This reduces input latency to the absolute minimum, measurable in competitive FPS titles where frame-perfect tracking matters.

**Omron Optical Switches** — Lamzu uses Omron optical micro-switches rated for 100 million clicks, eliminating the physical contact bounce that causes double-clicking on traditional mechanical switches. This is a structural reliability advantage over brands still using mechanical micro-switches at similar price tiers.

**Ultra-Lightweight Design (<50g)** — The Maya X weighs just 47g, the Atlantis Mini sits around 49g. These are among the lightest wireless gaming mice available, achieved through internal frame optimisation and minimal internal bracing. For flick-heavy FPS play (CS2, Valorant, Overwatch 2), sub-50g mice reduce fatigue during extended sessions and allow faster micro-adjustments.

**High-Precision Optical Sensors** — Lamzu mice use flagship-grade optical sensors (PixArt PMW3370 or equivalent class) with native DPI up to 26,000, zero acceleration/smoothing/filtering, and 400+ IPS tracking speed. Sensor performance is on par with Logitech HERO and Razer Focus Pro sensors at the hardware level.

**Magnetic Hall-Effect Switches (Keyboards)** — The Jet75 gaming keyboard uses Gateron Jade magnetic switches with rapid trigger support, allowing actuation point adjustment down to 0.1mm. This is the same switch technology used in Wooting and SteelSeries Apex Pro keyboards, but at a meaningfully lower NZD tier.
