## Brand Overview

Epomaker is a Chinese mechanical keyboard brand that has carved out a strong position in the enthusiast keyboard market, particularly in the Hall-effect magnetic switch segment. The brand is known for hot-swappable PCBs, wireless tri-mode connectivity, RGB backlighting, and distinctive colourway designs (gradient, pastel, and aesthetic-focused builds). Epomaker targets the enthusiast keyboard community — users who care about switch feel, hot-swap modularity, and keyboard aesthetics — but at value-tier pricing that undercuts established Western brands like Ducky, Keychron, and especially Wooting in the magnetic switch category. The brand has a loyal following in the mechanical keyboard community and is distributed through ExtremePC in New Zealand.

## Technology Highlights

**Hall-Effect Magnetic Switches** — Epomaker's HE (Hall Effect) series keyboards use magnetic switches that detect actuation through magnetic field changes rather than physical contact. This enables rapid trigger functionality — adjustable actuation points from 0.1mm to 4.0mm — allowing gamers to fine-tune key sensitivity per key. The HE75 V2 and HE68 models use Creamy Jade HE switches, which combine the smoothness of linear switches with magnetic precision. This technology was pioneered by Wooting at premium NZD pricing; Epomaker delivers it at value-tier prices.

**Hot-Swappable PCBs (5-pin/3-pin)** — Nearly all Epomaker keyboards feature hot-swappable sockets, meaning users can change switches without soldering. This is a significant advantage for enthusiasts who want to experiment with different switch profiles (linear, tactile, clicky) or replace worn switches. Supports both 5-pin and 3-pin switch standards, compatible with Kailh, Gateron, TTC, and Epomaker's own proprietary switches.

**Wireless Tri-Mode Connectivity** — Most Epomaker models support wired USB-C, 2.4GHz wireless (low-latency gaming), and Bluetooth (multi-device pairing). The 2.4GHz mode delivers polling rates suitable for competitive gaming, while Bluetooth allows seamless switching between desktop and laptop. Battery life typically exceeds 200 hours with backlighting off.

**Proprietary Switch Variants** — Epomaker develops its own switch variants including the Zebra Switch (Galaxy70), Flamingo Switch (HE65, QK81), Wisteria Linear (Cypher 96, RT85), and Creamy Jade (widely used across the lineup). These switches are tuned for specific models and are not available from other manufacturers, giving Epomaker keyboards a distinct typing feel.

**Informative OLED Screens (HE65 Mec)** — The HE65 Mec model includes an OLED display screen, showing battery status, connection mode, and customisable messages. This is a feature typically found on premium-tier keyboards ($200+ NZD) and is rare at Epomaker's value-tier pricing.
