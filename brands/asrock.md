## Brand Overview

ASRock is a Taiwanese motherboard and graphics card manufacturer founded in 2002 by former ASUS engineers. The brand is known for offering competitive specifications at value-tier pricing, particularly in the AMD Radeon graphics card segment. ASRock's Challenger and Phantom series GPUs use compact designs with effective cooling solutions, targeting budget-to-mid-tier PC builders. In the motherboard segment, ASRock covers everything from entry-level H-series chipsets to enthusiast X-series platforms. In New Zealand, ASRock GPUs are stocked through ExtremePC, with strong local warranty support.

## Technology Highlights

**Compact GPU Designs** — ASRock's Challenger series is known for compact form factors. The RX 9060 XT Challenger measures just 249mm × 132mm × 41mm, fitting in mid-tower and even some compact cases where larger triple-fan cards don't fit. Weight is kept low (645g for the RX 9060 XT Challenger), reducing GPU sag risk.

**Dual-Fan Cooling with 0dB Mode** — ASRock's dual-fan GPUs feature striped axial fan design with 0dB silent cooling — fans stop completely when GPU temperature drops below a threshold (typically 50°C), providing silent operation during light workloads (web browsing, office work, video playback). Ultra-fit heatpipe design maximises heat transfer in compact form factors.

**AMD RDNA 4 Architecture Support** — ASRock's RX 9060 XT and RX 9070 XT cards use AMD's RDNA 4 architecture with 3rd Gen Ray Tracing accelerators and 2nd Gen AI accelerators. Support for FSR 4 (ML-based upscaling) and AMD HYPR-RX (one-click optimisation) provides competitive gaming performance at value-tier pricing.

**Intel Arc GPU Support** — The ASRock Intel Arc B580 Challenger provides an entry-level dedicated GPU option with XeSS upscaling technology, targeting 1080p gaming at budget pricing.

**PCIe 5.0 Ready** — ASRock's newer GPU models feature PCIe 5.0 x16 interfaces, future-proofing for next-generation platforms.
