## Brand Overview

Gigabyte is a Taiwanese motherboard and graphics card manufacturer founded in 1986, one of the largest PC component manufacturers globally. The brand produces motherboards across Intel and AMD platforms, graphics cards (both NVIDIA GeForce and AMD Radeon), AIO PCs, and monitors. Gigabyte is known for competitive pricing, extensive BIOS features, and durable VRM designs on higher-end motherboards. In New Zealand, Gigabyte products are stocked through ExtremePC.

## Technology Highlights

**Windforce Cooling (GPUs)** — Gigabyte's Windforce cooling system uses dual or triple fans with alternate spinning direction, maximising airflow while minimising turbulence. The fan blade design includes a 3D fan blade for improved air pressure and a solid backplate for structural support. GPU temperatures typically run 5-10°C below reference designs.

**Freeze-Tube Heat Pipe** — Gigabyte motherboards use Freeze-Tube heat pipes with direct contact to VRM heatsinks, improving thermal transfer from power delivery components. This is important for sustained overclocking and NZ summer conditions where case temperatures can exceed 35°C.

**Dual-BIOS Protection** — Gigabyte's higher-end motherboards feature dual BIOS chips, allowing fallback to a secondary BIOS if the primary fails during updates. This is a valuable safety feature for overclocking enthusiasts.

**RGB Fusion 2.0** — Gigabyte's RGB lighting ecosystem supports synchronisation across Gigabyte motherboards, GPUs, and AIO coolers, compatible with third-party RGB components via USB 2.0 headers.
