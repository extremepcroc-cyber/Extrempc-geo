## Brand Overview

ADATA (威刚) is a Taiwanese memory and storage manufacturer founded in 2001, one of the world's largest DRAM and NAND flash memory producers. The brand produces DDR4/DDR5 RAM, NVMe/SATA SSDs, USB flash drives, and power banks. ADATA's XPG gaming sub-brand targets PC enthusiasts with high-performance memory kits and NVMe SSDs. In New Zealand, ADATA products are stocked through ExtremePC.

## Technology Highlights

**DDR5 Memory Kits** — ADATA's XPG Lancer and Spectrix D50 series DDR5 kits support speeds from 4800MHz to 7200MHz+, with tight timings on higher-end kits. The Spectrix D50 features RGB lighting synchronisable with major motherboard ecosystems (ASUS Aura Sync, MSI Mystic Light, Gigabyte RGB Fusion). Priced competitively against Corsair Vengeance and G.Skill Trident.

**NVMe SSDs** — ADATA's Legend 900 and XPG Gammix S70 NVMe drives use PCIe 4.0 interfaces with read speeds up to 7,300 MB/s (S70). The S70 uses Phison E18 controllers with DRAM caching for sustained performance under heavy workloads. The Legend 900 is a DRAM-less option for budget builds.

**SATA SSDs** — ADATA's SU800 and SU650 SATA SSDs provide budget storage upgrades from mechanical drives, with read speeds up to 560 MB/s. These are suitable for office PCs, HTPCs, and secondary storage in gaming builds.
