## Selling Points
**PCIe 5.0 storage on three M.2 slots.** Three PCIe 5.0 x4 M.2 slots let you pair a fast system drive with additional game or project drives. The limit to be aware of: heavy use of all three at once shares chipset bandwidth, so check the manual for slot sharing before filling every slot.

**Phantom Gaming Riptide positioning.** Riptide is ASRock's gaming-oriented sub-line below the Taichi and NOVA tiers. It fits builders who want X870 features and Wi-Fi 7 without moving to the enthusiast tier.

**192GB DDR5 ceiling with Wi-Fi 7.** Four DDR5 slots and up to 192GB cover heavy multitasking and editing, while Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 5.4 remove the need for an add-in wireless card.
