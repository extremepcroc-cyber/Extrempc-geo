## Selling Points
**Mini-ITX for small-form-factor builds.** At 170mm square, Mini-ITX boards suit compact cases such as the Jonsbo ITX range. The trade-offs are two DIMM slots and one PCIe slot on this size of board, so choose GPU and cooler for case clearance.

**Wi-Fi on board.** Small cases rarely have room for a separate wireless card, so the on-board Wi-Fi is useful.
