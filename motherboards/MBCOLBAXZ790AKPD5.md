## Selling Points
**Z790 is the overclocking-capable LGA 1700 chipset.** Z790 allows CPU overclocking on unlocked K-series processors, which the B760 boards in this range do not. This board suits a builder who has, or plans to buy, a Core i5, i7 or i9 K-series chip.

**DDR5 on LGA 1700.** The "D5" in the name means DDR5 memory. LGA 1700 boards come in both DDR4 and DDR5 versions, so check this before reusing old RAM.

**LGA 1700 sits at the end of its socket life.** Intel's newer Core Ultra processors use LGA 1851, so this board is the choice for 12th-14th Gen CPUs rather than an upgrade path to newer ones.
