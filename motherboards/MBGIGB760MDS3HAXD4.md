## Selling Points
**Four DDR4 slots up to 128GB.** Four DIMM slots on a Micro-ATX board let a builder start with two modules and add two later.

**Wi-Fi 6 and 2.5GbE.** Both wireless and a faster-than-gigabit wired port, useful with a 2.5G router or NAS.

**Two PCIe 4.0 M.2 slots.** Boot drive plus a storage drive.
