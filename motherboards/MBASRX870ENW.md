## Selling Points
**Four PCIe 5.0 x4 M.2 slots.** The NOVA carries one more PCIe 5.0 M.2 slot than the X870 boards in this range, which suits a builder who plans a boot drive, a scratch drive and two storage drives without an add-in card. As with any board with this many slots, check the manual for lane sharing when all four are in use.

**X870E chipset.** X870E is AMD's enhanced tier: more PCIe lanes and USB ports than plain X870. It is meant for builds with a high-end GPU plus several fast drives.

**Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4 and 192GB DDR5.** Wireless needs no extra card, and four DDR5 slots reach 192GB for editing, virtual machines or large projects.
