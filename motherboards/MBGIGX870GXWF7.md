## Selling Points
**Three PCIe 5.0 x4 M.2 slots.** With three M.2 slots on PCIe 5.0 x4, a builder can run a fast boot drive and add two more drives for projects or game libraries without a PCIe adapter card. The trade-off is that several M.2 slots share chipset bandwidth, so check the manual if you plan to fill all three with the fastest drives.

**Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 5.4.** The onboard wireless needs no add-in card. Wi-Fi 7 only delivers its full benefit with a Wi-Fi 7 router, so a typical Auckland home on a Wi-Fi 5 or 6 router will use the board's backwards-compatible modes.

**X870 on a standard ATX board.** X870 is AMD's higher chipset tier for AM5 with PCIe 5.0 support for the GPU and storage. It fits Ryzen 9000 CPUs, including X3D models, and keeps four DDR5 slots with up to 192GB capacity for editing and multitasking.
