## Selling Points
**Full-size ATX at B850 tier.** ATX gives more expansion-slot room than the Micro-ATX B850 boards, useful for a GPU plus a capture or sound card.

**Two PCIe 4.0 x4 M.2 slots and four DDR5 slots.** Two M.2 slots cover a boot drive and a games drive. Four DIMM slots reach 128GB. The trade-off is PCIe 4.0 storage, not PCIe 5.0 as on the X870 boards.

**Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth 5.4 included.** No add-in card needed for typical home networks.
