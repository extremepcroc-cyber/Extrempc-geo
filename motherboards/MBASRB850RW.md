## Selling Points
**Full-size ATX on B850.** The Riptide is an ATX board, so it has more expansion slots than the Micro-ATX B850 boards in the range, which matters if you plan a capture card or extra storage controller next to the GPU.

**Wi-Fi 7.** The product name lists Wi-Fi 7. It uses the full speed only on a Wi-Fi 7 router, and works with older routers in backward-compatible mode.

**Riptide is the gaming sub-line.** ASRock's Riptide styling sits between the entry Challenger and the enthusiast Taichi lines.
