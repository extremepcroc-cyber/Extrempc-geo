## Selling Points
**Four DDR4 slots on a Micro-ATX board.** Four DIMM slots let you start with two modules and add two later, a useful path for a build that begins at 16GB and grows.

**Wi-Fi AC built in.** "AC" in the product name refers to Wi-Fi 5. It covers typical home broadband use, but if your router is Wi-Fi 6 or newer and you want its full speed, the newer-generation Wi-Fi boards in the AM5 range are the alternative.

**R2 revision.** The R2 suffix marks the second hardware revision of the DS3H AC. Confirm the revision number when matching BIOS support for your CPU.
