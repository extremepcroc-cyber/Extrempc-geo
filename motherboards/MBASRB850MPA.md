## Selling Points
**Entry B850 in Micro-ATX.** The PRO-A line is ASRock's plain, lower-cost tier on B850: AM5 socket, DDR5 and Wi-Fi in a compact footprint.

**AM5 upgrade path.** AM5 supports Ryzen 7000 and 9000 CPUs, so a builder can start with a mid-range CPU and move up later without changing the board.
