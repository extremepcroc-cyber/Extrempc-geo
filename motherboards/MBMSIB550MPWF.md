## Selling Points
**AM4 platform that keeps existing DDR4 and Ryzen parts useful.** The B550M PRO-VDH Wi-Fi takes DDR4 memory and AM4 Ryzen processors, so a builder who already owns DDR4 modules does not have to rebuy them. That is the main reason to pick an AM4 board today rather than moving to AM5 and DDR5.

**Wi-Fi on the board.** The Wi-Fi in the product name removes the need for a separate PCIe or USB wireless adapter. This matters in Auckland flats and apartments where running Ethernet to the desk is not practical.

**Micro-ATX footprint.** The board fits Micro-ATX and ATX cases, which covers most compact and mid-tower cases stocked at ExtremePC. The trade-off of the smaller size is fewer expansion slots than a full ATX board.
