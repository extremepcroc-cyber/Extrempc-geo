## FAQ

- **Q: Are red switches good for typing, or only gaming?**
  A: Both. Red linears are light and smooth with no tactile bump, so they're easy on the fingers over long sessions and quiet by mechanical standards. If you prefer a tactile bump or a click for typing confirmation, this isn't the switch — the K500-M61's magnetic switch or a blue-switch board would suit better.

- **Q: Is it hot-swappable, or do I need to solder?**
  A: Hot-swappable. You can change to a different switch family without soldering, which is unusual at this price and the reason the board can outlive one switch preference.

- **Q: Does it have arrow keys?**
  A: Not physically — the 65% layout puts the arrows on the Fn layer, and the same for the F-row. If you use arrows constantly for spreadsheet or editor navigation, choose the 84-key K500A-B84 instead.

- **Q: Is it wireless?**
  A: No — wired USB-C. At this price tier a wired board removes the battery and dongle variables entirely. If you need wireless, the MCHOSE K87 or K99 V2 are the tri-mode options in the range.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: USB-C wired works on macOS out of the box. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
