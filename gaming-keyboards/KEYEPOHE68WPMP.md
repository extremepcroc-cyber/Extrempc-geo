## FAQ

- **Q: What's the difference between Magnetic Pink and the Clear Mag switch?**
  A: They're separate magnetic switch lines with different tuning. Clear Mag (HE68 Lite) has a clear housing that diffuses RGB; Magnetic Pink is tuned with a lighter feel. If you can't decide, test both at the Onehunga showroom — magnetic switch feel is the one spec a datasheet can't convey.

- **Q: Does it support rapid trigger?**
  A: Epomaker's HE series spec includes adjustable actuation and rapid trigger. If rapid trigger is a hard requirement, confirm the current firmware feature set with the Build Team before purchase.

- **Q: Why no arrow keys?**
