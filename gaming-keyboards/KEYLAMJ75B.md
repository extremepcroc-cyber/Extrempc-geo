For CS2 and Valorant that's faster counter-strafing and more consistent bunny-hopping.

- **75% layout with the function row intact** — 82 keys keeps the F-row, arrow cluster, and a media/browser block, dropping only the numpad. On a 27"–32" monitor with a full-size mouse pad, that's the layout that keeps the shortcuts without crowding the mouse.

- **Magnetic switches have no contact wear** — Hall Effect sensing reads the magnet's position instead of closing a metal contact, so there's no contact-oxidation ceiling of the kind mechanical switches carry (industry rating: 50–100 million keystrokes). Combined with hot-swap sockets, the board is built to outlast a switch preference.

- **Esports-tuned rather than feature-stuffed** — no display, no wireless, no gimmicks: wired for the shortest input chain, magnetic for the competitive feature set, and hot-swap sockets for tuning. If you want a board that does the competitive job without extras, that's the pitch.

## FAQ

- **Q: Is the Jade switch magnetic or mechanical?**
  A: Magnetic (Hall Effect). The Lamzu Jet75 uses Gateron Jade magnetic switches, which is what enables the adjustable actuation (down to 0.1mm) and rapid trigger.

- **Q: What's the difference between "Jade" here and the "Jade Pro" on other boards?**
  A: They're different revisions of the same Gateron Jade family — this board carries the Gaming variant, and Varmilo's Muse65 carries the Jade Pro. Both are magnetic with adjustable actuation; the tuning and revision differ. Testing both at Onehunga is the only reliable way to pick between them.

- **Q: Does it support rapid trigger?**
  A: Yes — the Jade magnetic switch with rapid trigger support is the core feature. Adjustable actuation goes down to 0.1mm.

- **Q: Why is it wired only at this price?**
  A: Wired removes the wireless variable entirely — no battery, no dongle, no latency question. That's consistent with Lamzu's performance-first approach. If you need wireless magnetic, Epomaker's G84 HE is the tri-mode option.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
