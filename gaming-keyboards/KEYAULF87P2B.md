## FAQ

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: Tri-mode. 2.4GHz via USB-C dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for a laptop or phone, and USB-C wired. Switch modes on the board itself; no driver needed for core functions.

- **Q: What's the difference between the F87 PRO V2 and the F75 MAX?**
  A: Layout. The F87 PRO V2 is TKL (87 keys) with the full function block; the F75 MAX is 75% (80 keys) and more compact. Choose the F87 PRO V2 if you want the extra function keys; choose the F75 MAX if the desk is tighter.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept LEOBOG, Kailh, Gateron, and TTC. Swap the LEOBOG X3 for a different linear or tactile without soldering.

- **Q: Will the transparent case show fingerprints?**
  A: A smoked/transparent acrylic-style body shows smudges more than a matte solid case does. A quick wipe with a microfiber cloth keeps it clear. It's the same behaviour as any transparent case material.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
