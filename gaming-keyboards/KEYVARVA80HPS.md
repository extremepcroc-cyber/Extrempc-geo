## FAQ

- **Q: How quiet is the Prestige Silent actually?**
  A: It's a dampened mechanical switch — noticeably quieter than a standard mechanical or a clicky switch, but not silent in the way a good membrane board is. For a shared bedroom or a microphone close to the board, it's the right choice in this range. If you need to verify against your specific situation, the Build Team can demonstrate the difference between the Voice and Silent variants at Onehunga.

- **Q: What's the difference between this and the Prestige Voice version?**
  A: Same board, same Hygge finish, different switch tuning: the Silent is dampened for reduced keystroke noise, the Voice is tuned for an audible character. Both are mechanical Kailh switches in the Prestige line.

- **Q: Is this board magnetic or mechanical?**
  A: Mechanical. The magnetic Varmilo models are the Victory 67 and Muse65. The VA80 Hygge uses a Kailh mechanical switch — no rapid trigger, but a lighter press feel than HE switches and the quieter Prestige Silent tuning.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. The sockets accept the standard switch families, so the Prestige Silent can be swapped for a different switch type without soldering.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: USB-C wired works on macOS out of the box. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
