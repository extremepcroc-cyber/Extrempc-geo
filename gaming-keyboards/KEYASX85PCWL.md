## FAQ

- **Q: Does the white case yellow over time?**
  A: A warm-toned ABS case tends to show age more gracefully than a stark white one, but it will still mark and can discolour over 12–24 months in a warm, humid Auckland climate, particularly under direct sun. A wipe-down routine and a desk position out of direct afternoon sun keeps it looking right for longer.

- **Q: Does it have Bluetooth?**
  A: No — 2.4GHz wireless plus USB-C wired. For Bluetooth multi-device pairing, look at the AULA F75 MAX or MCHOSE K87 in the range.

- **Q: Are the switches linear or clicky?**

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
