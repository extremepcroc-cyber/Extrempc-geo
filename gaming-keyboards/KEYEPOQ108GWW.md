## FAQ

- **Q: Is it 2.4GHz or Bluetooth, or both?**
  A: The QK108 runs 2.4GHz wireless plus USB-C wired. If you need Bluetooth for pairing to a tablet or phone, Epomaker's G84 HE and other models in the range carry tri-mode.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. Standard sockets accept the main switch families, so you can swap the Wisteria linears for tactiles or a different linear without soldering — including mixing switch types across the numpad and alphas.

- **Q: Is it good for gaming?**
  A: It's a wireless mechanical board — fine for general gaming, MMO, and productivity. It doesn't offer adjustable actuation or rapid trigger, so if you play CS2 or Valorant competitively, an HE board (such as Epomaker's G84 HE) is the technical pick.
