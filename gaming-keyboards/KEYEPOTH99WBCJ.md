## FAQ

- **Q: Is it 2.4GHz or Bluetooth, or both?**
  A: The TH99 runs 2.4GHz wireless plus USB-C wired. If you need Bluetooth for pairing to a tablet or phone, Epomaker's tri-mode models (such as the G84 HE) are the alternative.

- **Q: Is it good for gaming?**
  A: It's a wireless mechanical board — fine for general gaming, MMO, and productivity. It doesn't offer adjustable actuation or rapid trigger, so for competitive FPS an HE board is the technical pick.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. Standard sockets accept the main switch families, so the switch choice isn't locked — including mixing switch types across the numpad and alphas.

- **Q: Does it support QMK/VIA remapping?**
  A: The TH99 is not listed as a QMK/VIA model — that's the Galaxy 100's feature. The TH99 carries the standard remapping software. If deep customisation is essential, choose the Galaxy 100.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: USB-C wired and the 2.4GHz dongle both work on macOS. Key remapping software is Windows-first on most Epomaker models.
