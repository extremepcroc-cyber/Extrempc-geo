## FAQ

- **Q: Is it wired only, or is there wireless too?**
  A: Wired USB-C only. That's a deliberate design choice at this tier — no battery, no dongle, no Bluetooth. If you need wireless, the AULA F75 MAX (KEYAULF75MWS) is the same 75% layout with tri-mode connectivity.

- **Q: What does "pre-lubed" actually mean for the typing feel?**
  A: The switch stems and housings are factory-coated with a thin layer of lubricant before assembly. The practical result: the out-of-box typing is smooth and quiet, without the scratchiness of a dry value board. Most enthusiast mods spend a weekend doing this by hand; the Reaper ships already done.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept LEOBOG, Kailh, Gateron, and TTC families. Swap the Reapers for a heavier tactile or a lighter linear without soldering.

- **Q: Will it fit a 27" monitor setup?**
  A: 75% is the sweet spot for 24"–27" monitors. You keep the F-row and arrow cluster, and the board is narrow enough to leave full-size mouse pad room for wide mouse movements.

- **Q: What's the difference between the Reaper and the Star Vector?**
  A: Different switch lines. The Reaper is LEOBOG's pre-lubed linear; the Star Vector is AULA's own proprietary switch on the F75 MAX. Both are mechanical linears; the practical difference is the out-of-box feel — the Reaper is smoother and quieter straight out of the box, the Star Vector is drier and more "adjustable by lube."

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: USB-C wired works on macOS out of the box. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
