- **Rapid trigger is the competitive reason to go magnetic** — a key re-actuates after as little as 0.1mm of movement in the opposite direction rather than requiring a full return past a fixed reset point. In CS2 and Valorant that's faster counter-strafing and more consistent bunny-hopping.

- **Magnetic switches have no contact wear** — Hall Effect sensing reads the magnet's position instead of closing a metal contact, so there's no contact-oxidation ceiling of the kind mechanical switches carry (industry rating: 50–100 million keystrokes).
