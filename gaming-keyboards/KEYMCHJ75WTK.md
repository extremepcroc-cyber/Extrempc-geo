## FAQ

- **Q: What does "Magnetic God" mean — is it a marketing term?**
  A: It's the product name of Kailh's magnetic (Hall Effect) switch line. The relevant substance is that it's Kailh's magnetic switch, with the adjustable actuation and rapid trigger capability that magnetic sensing enables. If you want the exact actuation-force and travel figures, ask the Build Team to confirm against the current spec sheet.

- **Q: Does it support rapid trigger?**
  A: MCHOSE's magnetic-switch keyboards support adjustable actuation and rapid trigger. If rapid trigger is a hard requirement, confirm the current firmware feature set with the Build Team before purchase.

- **Q: Why is it wired only?**
  A: At this tier, wired keeps the price down and removes the battery and dongle variables. If you need wireless magnetic, Epomaker's G84 HE is the tri-mode option.

- **Q: What's the topographic design — is it printed on the keycaps?**
  A: The topographic pattern is part of the board's keycap and side detailing, giving it a designed rather than flat appearance. If you need to know whether the pattern covers the keycaps, the case sides, or both on the current production run, ask the Build Team to show you a unit at Onehunga.

- **Q: Is it good for typing as well as gaming?**
  A: Yes, with one caveat: HE switches typically need a heavier return spring to overcome the magnet-sensor interaction, so a "light" HE switch can feel heavier on the return than a comparably-rated mechanical linear. Try it in store if you type for long stretches.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: USB-C wired works on macOS out of the box. The actuation and rapid-trigger software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
