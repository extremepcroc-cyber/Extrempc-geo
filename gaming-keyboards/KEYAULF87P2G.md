## FAQ

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: Tri-mode. 2.4GHz via USB-C dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for a laptop or phone, and USB-C wired. Switch modes on the board itself; no driver needed for core functions.

- **Q: What's the difference between the grey and the black F87 PRO V2?**
  A: Two differences. The grey carries the Greywood V4 switch; the black carries the LEOBOG X3. The case also differs — the grey is a solid gradient, the black is a smoked transparent. Same layout, same tri-mode wireless.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept LEOBOG, Kailh, Gateron, and TTC. Swap the Greywood V4 for a different linear or tactile without soldering.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**
  A: This is a low-stock colourway at the moment — only a few units at Onehunga. If you want it, check with the store before assuming it'll still be there next week.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
