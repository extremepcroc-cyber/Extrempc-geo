## FAQ

- **Q: Is it 2.4GHz or Bluetooth, or both?**
  A: Both — tri-mode. 2.4GHz via USB-C dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for a laptop or phone, and USB-C wired. Switch between modes on the board itself; no driver needed for the core functions.

- **Q: Are the switches actually hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept the major mechanical switch families (LEOBOG, Kailh, Gateron, TTC). Pull the Star Vector switches with a switch puller, drop in your preferred linears or tactiles, done.

- **Q: What's the difference between this and the white F75 MAX?**
  A: Colour only. Same layout, same Star Vector switches, same tri-mode wireless. The white has a light side-print; the black has a dark side-print. Pick the one that matches the desk.

- **Q: Battery life in 2.4GHz mode?**
  A: AULA doesn't publish an exact runtime figure in our BC listing — practical experience is a full charge covers several days of normal use, and with RGB at max plus long daily sessions you'll be topping up more often. If exact battery spec matters, ask the Build Team and they'll confirm against the current spec sheet.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
