## FAQ

- **Q: What is the Epomaker Core Switch?**
  A: It's Epomaker's in-house switch line used across the Magcore series. Because it's a proprietary line, it isn't interchangeable with other manufacturers' switches by name — but the board's hot-swap sockets accept the standard 5-pin/3-pin families, so you're not locked in. If you want the exact switch specifications (actuation force, travel, and whether rapid trigger features are supported), ask the Build Team to confirm against the current spec sheet.

- **Q: Is it 2.4GHz or Bluetooth, or both?**
  A: The Magcore65 Lite runs 2.4GHz wireless plus USB-C wired. If you need Bluetooth for pairing to a tablet or phone, the G84 HE and other models in the Epomaker range carry tri-mode.

- **Q: Why no arrow keys?**
  A: The 65% layout puts arrow functions on a layer. Fine for gaming and general typing; for heavy spreadsheet or editor navigation, a 75% or TKL board is the better fit.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes — the sockets accept the standard 5-pin/3-pin switch families, so the switch choice isn't locked to the Core Switch.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**
  A: Low stock at the moment — a few units at Onehunga. Confirm availability with the store before planning around it.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: USB-C wired and the 2.4GHz dongle both work on macOS. Key remapping software is Windows-first on most Epomaker models; check current macOS support before buying if deep remapping matters.
