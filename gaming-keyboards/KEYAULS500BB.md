## FAQ

- **Q: Are blue switches loud?**
  A: Yes — blue switches have an audible click plus a tactile bump, which is the point for people who like them. In a shared bedroom, open-plan office, or a house where people are on calls nearby, that noise is a real consideration. If quiet matters more, the F108 PRO's Caramel Latte linears are the better fit.

- **Q: Is it hot-swappable, or do I need to solder?**
  A: Hot-swappable. You can pull the blue switches and drop in a different family without soldering — which is unusual at this price and the reason the board can outlive one switch preference.

- **Q: Does it have wireless?**
  A: No — this is a wired USB board. That's deliberate at this price tier: no battery, no dongle, no charging. If you need wireless, the AULA F108 PRO or F75 MAX are the wireless options in the same full-size and 75% layouts.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Wired USB works on macOS out of the box. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.

- **Q: Is 104 keys the right layout for gaming?**
  A: For gaming alone, a 75% or TKL keeps more desk room for the mouse. The S500's full-size layout is best when you also do numpad-dependent work — that's the trade-off versus a compact board.
