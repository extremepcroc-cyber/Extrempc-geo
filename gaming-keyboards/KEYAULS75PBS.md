## FAQ

- **Q: What's the difference between the S75 Pro and the F75 MAX?**
  A: The S75 Pro has 84 keys with a dedicated media/browser row on the right; the F75 MAX has 80 keys without that row. Same Star Vector switches, same tri-mode wireless. Choose the S75 Pro if you want the extra media keys; choose the F75 MAX if you want the tighter footprint.

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: Tri-mode. 2.4GHz via USB-C dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for a laptop or phone, and USB-C wired. Switch modes on the board itself; no driver needed for core functions.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept LEOBOG, Kailh, Gateron, and TTC. Swap the Star Vector for a different linear or tactile without soldering.

- **Q: Do I need the media keys, or is the F75 MAX enough?**
  A: If your daily workflow leans on play/pause, volume, and mic-mute for calls or streaming, the dedicated media row on the S75 Pro saves you from reaching for a Fn-combo. If you rarely use those keys, the F75 MAX's tighter footprint is the better fit.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
