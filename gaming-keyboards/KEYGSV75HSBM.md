## Selling Points

- **Stealth Black is the finish that makes the RGB the only colour on the desk** — the Mercury case is the design element at this tier, and in Stealth Black it recedes so the per-key lighting does the work. For a dark-themed build where the keyboard is meant to look considered rather than loud, that's the right call.

- **Gateron Magnetic Jade Pro — a documented switch, not a marketing name** — Gateron's Jade Pro line has published characteristics, so you can compare actuation force and travel against your reference switch before buying. Paired with a GravaStar Mercury case, both halves of the purchase are things you can actually evaluate in advance.

- **Rapid trigger with adjustable actuation is the competitive core** — the key re-actuates after as little as 0.1mm of movement in the opposite direction instead of requiring a full return past a fixed reset point. In CS2 and Valorant that's faster counter-strafing and more consistent bunny-hopping.

- **No contact wear** — Hall Effect sensing reads the magnet's position rather than closing a metal contact, so there's no contact-oxidation ceiling of the kind mechanical switches carry (industry rating: 50–100 million keystrokes). On a board built to last years, the mechanism simply isn't there.

- **Wired and 80 keys — the two conservative choices that suit a competitive battlestation** — no battery, no dongle, nothing to check before a ranked session, and the F-row and arrow cluster stay physically present.

## FAQ

- **Q: Does it support rapid trigger?**
  A: Yes — the Gateron Jade Pro is a magnetic (Hall Effect) switch and the board supports rapid trigger with adjustable actuation. For the exact adjustable range on the current firmware, the Build Team can confirm from the live spec sheet at Onehunga.

- **Q: Is it wired only?**
  A: Yes — USB-C wired. Wired removes the wireless variable entirely, which is standard for competitive magnetic boards. The GravaStar Mercury K1 Pro in the same series is the wireless option.

- **Q: Is $349 justified over a cheaper magnetic board?**
  A: Honestly, the functional difference between this and a value-tier magnetic TKL is smaller than the price difference — both give you rapid trigger and adjustable actuation. What the extra buys is the Gateron Jade Pro switch and the Mercury case. If neither matters to you, a cheaper board will do the same job, and the Build Team will say so.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes — the sockets accept the standard switch families, so the Jade Pro can be swapped without soldering.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**
  A: One unit at Onehunga at the moment. A phone call confirms whether it's still on the shelf.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: USB-C wired works on macOS out of the box. The actuation and rapid-trigger software is Windows-first, so on Mac you'd be on the system defaults.
