## FAQ

- **Q: What's the Zebra switch like compared to Creamy Jade?**
  A: They're separate in-house Epomaker lines — Zebra is its own tuning rather than a variant of Creamy Jade. Both are linear-class switches; the difference is in the spring weight and sound signature. If you can't choose from a datasheet, test both at the Onehunga showroom.

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: Tri-mode. 2.4GHz via dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for multi-device pairing, and USB-C wired.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. Standard 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept Kailh, Gateron, TTC, and Epomaker's own lines. Swap the Zebra switches without soldering.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**
  A: Low stock at the moment — a couple of units at Onehunga. Confirm availability with the store before planning around it.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first on most Epomaker models.
