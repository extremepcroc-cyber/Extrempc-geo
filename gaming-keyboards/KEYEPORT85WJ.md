## FAQ

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: Tri-mode. 2.4GHz via dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for multi-device pairing, and USB-C wired. Switch modes on the board or via the companion software.

- **Q: What's special about Creamy Jade switches?**
  A: Creamy Jade is Epomaker's most widely used in-house switch line — a smooth linear with a warmer sound signature than a standard dry linear. Because it appears across the Epomaker range, it's the switch most community reviews of the brand are based on.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. Standard 5-pin sockets accept Kailh, Gateron, TTC, and Epomaker's own lines. Swap the Creamy Jade for a tactile or clicky without soldering.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**
  A: Low stock at the moment — a single unit at Onehunga for this switch variant. Confirm availability with the store before planning around it.

- **Q: What's the difference between this and the G84?**
  A: The G84 is 84 keys; the RT85 is 82 keys. Both are TKL-class boards with Creamy Jade. Layout details and the colourway differ. If key count isn't decisive for you, compare them side by side at Onehunga.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first on most Epomaker models.
