## FAQ

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: Tri-mode. 2.4GHz via USB-C dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for a laptop or phone, and USB-C wired. Switch modes on the board itself; no driver needed for core functions.

- **Q: What's the difference between the white, black, and grey F87 PRO V2?**
  A: Colour and switch. The white carries the Star Vector; the black carries the LEOBOG X3 in a smoked transparent case; the grey carries the Greywood V4 in a solid gradient case. Same layout, same tri-mode wireless across all three.

- **Q: Will the white case yellow over time?**
  A: ABS plastic in a warm, humid Auckland climate can yellow over 12–24 months of heavy use, especially under direct sun. Keep it out of direct afternoon sun and it'll stay brighter longer. It's the same behaviour as any white ABS case.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept LEOBOG, Kailh, Gateron, and TTC. Swap the Star Vector for a different linear or tactile without soldering.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**
  A: This is a low-stock colourway at the moment — only a couple of units at Onehunga. If you want it, check with the store before assuming it'll still be there next week.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
