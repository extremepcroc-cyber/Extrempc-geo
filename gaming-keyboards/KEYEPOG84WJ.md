## FAQ

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: Tri-mode. 2.4GHz via dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for multi-device pairing (laptop, phone, tablet), and USB-C wired. Switch modes on the board or via the companion software.

- **Q: What's special about Creamy Jade switches?**
  A: Creamy Jade is one of Epomaker's most widely used in-house switch lines — a smooth linear with a warmer sound signature than a standard dry linear. Because it appears across the Epomaker range, it's the switch most community reviews of the brand are based on.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept Kailh, Gateron, TTC, and Epomaker's own switch lines. Swap the Creamy Jade linears for tactiles or clickies without soldering.

- **Q: Is it good for competitive FPS?**
  A: It's a mechanical board with a fixed actuation point — fine for general gaming, MMO, and productivity, but it doesn't offer the adjustable actuation and rapid trigger that Hall-effect boards do. If you play CS2 or Valorant at a competitive level, the G84 HE (KEYEPOG84HWJ) in the same family is the technical pick.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first on most Epomaker models; check the current software support for macOS before buying if deep remapping matters to you.
