## FAQ

- **Q: Why is the Pro more expensive than the standard G84?**
  A: It's the higher revision in the family — updated internals and the distinctive purple colourway, at 81 keys versus the standard's 84. If neither the revision nor the colour matters to you, the standard G84 delivers the same switch at the value price.

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: Tri-mode. 2.4GHz via dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for multi-device pairing, and USB-C wired. Switch modes on the board or via the companion software.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept Kailh, Gateron, TTC, and Epomaker's own switch lines. Swap the Creamy Jade linears for tactiles or clickies without soldering.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**
  A: This is a low-stock colourway — a single unit at Onehunga at the moment. If you want the purple specifically, check with the store before assuming it'll still be available.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first on most Epomaker models; check current macOS support before buying if deep remapping matters.
