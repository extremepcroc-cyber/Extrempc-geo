## FAQ

- **Q: What's the difference between Wisteria V2 and Creamy Jade?**
  A: They're separate Epomaker in-house switch lines. Creamy Jade is the brand's most widely used linear with a warmer sound signature; Wisteria V2 is the updated revision of the Wisteria line with a tighter linear feel. Both are smooth linears — the difference is tuning, and it's the kind of thing worth testing in person at Onehunga.

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: Tri-mode. 2.4GHz via dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for multi-device pairing, and USB-C wired. Switch modes on the board or via the companion software.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. Standard 5-pin sockets accept Kailh, Gateron, TTC, and Epomaker's own lines. Swap the Wisteria V2 for a different linear, tactile, or clicky without soldering.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**
  A: Low stock at the moment — a single unit at Onehunga for this switch variant. Confirm availability with the store before planning around it.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first on most Epomaker models.
