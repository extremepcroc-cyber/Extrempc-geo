## FAQ

- **Q: Is this a magnetic (Hall Effect) keyboard?**
  A: No — Razer Analog Optical switches, which read a beam of light rather than a magnetic field. The capabilities overlap (adjustable actuation, rapid trigger, no contact wear) but the underlying technology differs. For magnetic specifically, see the Varmilo Victory/Muse65 or MCHOSE magnetic boards.

- **Q: What does the 8KHz polling rate actually do?**
  A: It reports key states 8,000 times per second — a 0.125ms interval — versus 1,000Hz on a standard gaming keyboard. Whether that's perceptible is debated; it's the highest polling tier available and part of what this board buys.

- **Q: How big is the desk saving versus the full-size version?**
  A: The TKL drops the numpad and the associated width — roughly the width of a number pad. If you run a full-size mouse pad and flick the mouse widely, that's the difference between the pad fitting and the keyboard crowding it.

- **Q: Is there a wireless version?**
  A: This model is wired USB-C. Wired is standard for flagship competitive boards because it removes the wireless variable.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**

- **Q: What software do I need?**
