## FAQ

- **Q: Does it have Bluetooth, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: The AU75 is 2.4GHz wireless plus USB-C wired. If you need Bluetooth for switching between a laptop and phone, the AULA F75 MAX (KEYAULF75MWS) is the tri-mode equivalent in the same 75% size.

- **Q: Can I swap the switches if the yellow accents aren't my style?**
  A: The switches are hot-swappable (5-pin/3-pin sockets, LEOBOG/Kailh/Gateron/TTC compatible), but the case colour is fixed. If black-yellow isn't for you, the F75 MAX or Nova75 carry the same platform in neutral colours.

- **Q: Is 81 keys different from 80?**
  A: One extra key in the arrangement. AULA lists the AU75 as an 81-key 75% layout; the F75 MAX is listed as 80 keys. Functionally it's the same form factor — the difference is one key position.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept LEOBOG, Kailh, Gateron, and TTC. Swap the Star Vector for a different linear or tactile without soldering.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: USB-C wired works on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
