## FAQ

- **Q: Is the Ice switch magnetic or mechanical?**
  A: Mechanical. The K87 is the mechanical side of MCHOSE's keyboard range — the magnetic (Hall Effect) models are the Mix 87, Jet 75, and Ace 68. That means no rapid trigger on the K87, but also none of the heavier return-spring feel that HE switches can have.

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode?**
  A: Yes — wired USB-C, 2.4GHz wireless, and Bluetooth. Switch between them on the board; no driver needed for the core functions.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. Standard sockets accept the main switch families, so the Ice switch can be swapped for a different linear, tactile, or clicky without soldering.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**
  A: Low stock at the moment — a couple of units at Onehunga. Confirm availability with the store before planning around it.

- **Q: Is it good for competitive FPS?**
  A: It's a mechanical board with a fixed actuation point — fine for general gaming and MMO. If you play CS2 or Valorant competitively and want rapid trigger, the magnetic models (Mix 87, Jet 75) are the technical pick.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first.
