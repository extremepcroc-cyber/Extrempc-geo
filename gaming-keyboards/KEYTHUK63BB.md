## FAQ

- **Q: Are brown switches good for gaming as well as typing?**
  A: Yes — brown is a tactile switch with a light enough press for gaming and a bump for typing confirmation. It's the middle setting between red (light linear, fastest) and blue (clicky, most feedback). If you want maximum speed, red; if you want the click, blue.

- **Q: Does it have arrow keys?**
  A: Not physically — the 60% layout puts the arrow cluster on the Fn layer, and the same for the F-row. If you navigate with arrows constantly, a 65% or 75% board with them physically present is the better fit — the Machenike K500-B68 or K500A-B84.

- **Q: Is it hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes — the sockets accept the standard switch families, so the brown switches can be swapped for a linear or clicky set without soldering. That's unusual at this price.

- **Q: Is the backlight per-key RGB?**
  A: The board has RGB backlighting. If the exact per-key behaviour matters to you, the Build Team can confirm against the current spec sheet — the range has both per-key and zone-based lighting on different models.

- **Q: Is it wireless?**
  A: No — wired USB-C. At this price tier, wired removes the battery and dongle variables. For wireless, the Attack Shark X85 Pro or AULA F75 MAX in the range are the options.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: USB-C wired works on macOS out of the box. Key remapping software is Windows-first, so on Mac you'd be on the system defaults.
