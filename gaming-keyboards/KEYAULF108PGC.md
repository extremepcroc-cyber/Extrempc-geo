## FAQ

- **Q: Why full-size when all the 2026 trend is compact 65%/75%?**
  A: Because you need the numpad. For spreadsheets, CAD coordinate entry, and financial modelling, the numpad is a daily necessity, not a legacy feature. The F108 PRO is the value-tier full-size mechanical with tri-mode — a combination that's hard to find at this price.

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: Tri-mode. 2.4GHz via USB-C dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for a laptop or phone, and USB-C wired. Switch modes on the board itself; no driver needed for core functions.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept LEOBOG, Kailh, Gateron, and TTC. Swap the Caramel Latte linears for tactiles or a different linear without soldering.

- **Q: Battery life in 2.4GHz mode?**
  A: AULA doesn't publish an exact runtime figure in our BC listing — a full charge covers several days of normal use, and with RGB at max plus long daily sessions you'll top up more often. If exact battery spec matters, ask the Build Team and they'll confirm against the current spec sheet. Wired mode draws from the USB-C cable so battery isn't a factor there.

- **Q: Will it fit a 27" monitor setup?**
  A: Full-size is the widest layout — it needs a deeper desk than a 75% board. If your desk is under 70cm deep or you run a full-size mouse pad for wide mouse movements, measure first. For a 30"+ desk with a 27" monitor, it fits comfortably.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
