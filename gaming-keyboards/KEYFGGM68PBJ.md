## FAQ

- **Q: Is the Jade Esport Pro a magnetic switch?**
  A: The Jade family name appears across both Gateron's magnetic lines and its mechanical lines, so the platform on this specific revision is worth confirming rather than assuming. If adjustable actuation and rapid trigger are requirements for you, ask the Build Team to verify the live spec — and if they are, the MAD68 HE Flagship V2 in the same range is the board specified for it.

- **Q: What's the difference between this and the MAD68 HE?**
  A: The switch. This Pro carries the Gateron Jade Esport Pro; the HE Flagship V2 carries the Magneto magnetic switch with adjustable actuation and rapid trigger. Same 68-key layout, same price — it's a switch-platform decision.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes — the sockets accept the standard switch families, so the Jade Esport Pro can be changed later without soldering.

- **Q: Why no arrow keys or F-row?**

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
