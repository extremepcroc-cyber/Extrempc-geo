## FAQ

- **Q: Is this a magnetic switch or a regular mechanical switch?**
  A: Magnetic (Hall Effect). The actuation is read by a sensor rather than closed by a metal contact, which is what enables adjustable actuation and rapid trigger. Note the BC listing spells it "Magnetic Swtich" — that's a typo in the product name, the switch is magnetic.

- **Q: Does it support rapid trigger?**
  A: The board carries magnetic switches, which is the technology that enables rapid trigger. If rapid trigger is a hard requirement for your play, confirm the current firmware feature set with the Build Team before purchase — they can verify against the live spec sheet at Onehunga.

- **Q: Why no arrow keys or F-row?**
  A: The 60% layout puts the F-row, arrows, and other functions on the Fn layer. That's the trade for the smallest footprint. For spreadsheet, IDE, or DAW work, a 75% or TKL is the better fit.

- **Q: Is the magnetic feel different from a mechanical switch?**
  A: Yes. HE switches typically need a heavier return spring to overcome the magnet-sensor interaction, so a "light" HE switch can feel heavier on the return than a comparably-rated mechanical linear. For gaming the faster reset is the payoff.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: USB-C wired works on macOS out of the box. The actuation and rapid-trigger software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
