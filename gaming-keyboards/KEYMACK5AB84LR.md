## FAQ

- **Q: Does it have arrow keys?**
  A: Yes — the arrow cluster is physically present on this layout, along with the F-row and a function block. That's the difference versus the cheaper 60% and 65% boards in Machenike's range.

- **Q: Are red switches good for typing, or only gaming?**
  A: Both. Red linears are light and smooth with no tactile bump, easy on the fingers over long sessions, and quiet by mechanical standards. If you want a tactile bump or a click for typing confirmation, this isn't the switch.

- **Q: Is it hot-swappable, or do I need to solder?**
  A: Hot-swappable. You can move to a different switch family without soldering — unusual at this price, and the reason the board can outlive one switch preference.

- **Q: Is the backlight per-key RGB?**
  A: This model is listed with an LED backlight rather than per-key RGB. If per-key RGB is a requirement, check the other Machenike models in the range, or confirm the lighting spec with the Build Team before purchase.

- **Q: Is it wireless?**
  A: No — wired USB-C. At this price tier, wired removes the battery and dongle variables. For wireless, the MCHOSE K87 or K99 V2 are the tri-mode options in the range.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: USB-C wired works on macOS out of the box. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
