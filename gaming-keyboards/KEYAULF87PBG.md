## FAQ

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: Tri-mode. 2.4GHz via USB-C dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for a laptop or phone, and USB-C wired. Switch modes on the board itself; no driver needed for core functions.

- **Q: What's the difference between the F87 Pro and the F87 PRO V2?**
  A: The V2 is the updated revision — newer switch tuning (X3 / Greywood V4 / Star Vector depending on colourway) and a refined case (smoked transparent or gradient). The F87 Pro is the first generation with the LEOBOG Greywood switch and a black-grey two-tone case. Same layout, same tri-mode. If the V2 isn't a requirement, the F87 Pro is the value pick.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept LEOBOG, Kailh, Gateron, and TTC. Swap the Greywood for a different linear or tactile without soldering.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**
  A: This is a low-stock unit at the moment — a couple of units at Onehunga. If you want it, check with the store before assuming it'll still be there next week.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
