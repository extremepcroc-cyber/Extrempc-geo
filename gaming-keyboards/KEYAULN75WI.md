## FAQ

- **Q: How is the Ice Shadow different from the Star Vector on the F75 MAX?**
  A: They're separate in-house switch lines with different tuning. AULA developed Star Vector for the F75 MAX / S75 Pro and Ice Shadow for the Nova75. Both are mechanical linears; the difference is in the spring weight and the out-of-box feel. If you can't decide, test both at the Onehunga showroom — switch feel is the one spec a datasheet can't convey.

- **Q: Is it really tri-mode, or just 2.4GHz?**
  A: Tri-mode. 2.4GHz via USB-C dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for a laptop or phone, and USB-C wired. Switch modes on the board itself; no driver needed for core functions.

- **Q: Will the white case yellow over time?**
  A: ABS plastic in a warm, humid Auckland climate can yellow over 12–24 months of heavy use, especially under direct sun. A light desk position out of direct afternoon sun slows the process. It's the same behaviour as any white ABS case.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: Yes. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept LEOBOG, Kailh, Gateron, and TTC. Swap the Ice Shadow for a different linear or tactile without soldering.

- **Q: How much stock is there?**
  A: This is a low-stock colourway at the moment — a few units at Onehunga. If you want it, check with the store before assuming it'll still be there next week.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac. Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
