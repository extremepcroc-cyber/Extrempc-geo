## FAQ

- **Q: Is this a magnetic (Hall Effect) keyboard?**
  A: No — Razer Analog Optical switches, which read a beam of light rather than a magnetic field. Capabilities overlap (adjustable actuation, rapid trigger, no contact wear) but the technology differs. For magnetic specifically, see Varmilo's Victory/Muse65 or the MCHOSE magnetic boards.

- **Q: Does white get dirty or yellow?**
  A: A white ABS or PBT case will show dust and hand oils more than a black one, and ABS can yellow over 12–24 months in a warm, humid climate, especially under direct sun. A regular wipe-down and a desk position out of direct afternoon sun keep it looking new longer.

- **Q: What does the 8KHz polling rate actually do?**
  A: It reports key states 8,000 times per second — a 0.125ms interval — versus 1,000Hz on a standard gaming keyboard. Whether that's perceptible is debated; it's the highest tier available.

- **Q: Is there a wireless version?**
  A: This model is wired USB-C. Wired is standard for flagship competitive boards because it removes the wireless variable.
