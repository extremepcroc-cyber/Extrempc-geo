## FAQ

- **Q: Is it 2.4GHz or Bluetooth, or both?**
  A: Both — tri-mode. 2.4GHz via USB-C dongle for low-latency gaming, Bluetooth for connecting to a laptop or phone, and USB-C wired. Switch between modes on the board itself; no driver needed for the core functions.

- **Q: Are the switches actually hot-swappable, or do I need a soldering iron?**
  A: Hot-swappable. 5-pin/3-pin sockets accept the major mechanical switch families (LEOBOG, Kailh, Gateron, TTC). Pull the Star Vector switches out with a switch puller, drop in your preferred linears or tactiles, done.

- **Q: Will the white get yellowing?**
  A: ABS plastic in a warm, humid Auckland climate can yellow over 12–24 months of heavy use, especially under direct sun. If that's a concern, a light desk position out of direct afternoon sun and a light-coloured desk mat slow the process. It's the same behaviour as any white ABS case.

- **Q: Battery life in 2.4GHz mode?**
  A: AULA doesn't publish an exact runtime figure in our BC listing — practical experience is a full charge covers several days of normal use, and with RGB at max plus long daily sessions you'll be topping up more often. If exact battery spec matters to you, ask the Build Team and they'll confirm against the current spec sheet. Wired mode draws from the USB-C cable so battery isn't a factor there.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: Bluetooth and USB-C wired both work on macOS out of the box. The 2.4GHz dongle also works on Mac — plug it into the USB-C port (or a hub). Key remapping software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.

- **Q: What's the polling rate?**
  A: AULA lists 2.4GHz mode as low-latency gaming-grade; the exact polling rate spec isn't published in our BC listing. If 1000Hz polling is a hard requirement for your play, confirm with the Build Team before purchase — they can verify the current spec sheet.
