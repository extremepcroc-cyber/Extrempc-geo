## FAQ

- **Q: Does it have RGB?**
  A: No — this board has no backlight. That's a deliberate part of the design and a reason it lands at this price with VIA support. If RGB matters, the CIDOO V87 in the range has per-key lighting.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
  A: No — the Pearl White switches are fixed. If you want to change switch families later, the V87 has hot-swap sockets. If you specifically want to confirm the switch specification for your intended use, ask the Build Team to check the current sheet.

- **Q: What is VIA, and do I need to code anything?**
  A: VIA is a browser-based configurator with a visual keymap editor — you click a key, pick what it should do, and save. No code required for remapping, layers, or macros. QMK is the deeper code-level firmware underneath it, and most users never need it.

- **Q: Does it have Bluetooth?**
  A: No — 2.4GHz wireless plus USB-C wired. For Bluetooth multi-device pairing, look at CIDOO's V87 or the AULA and MCHOSE tri-mode boards in the range.

- **Q: Why no arrow keys?**
  A: The 65% layout puts them on the Fn layer — and via VIA you decide which physical keys do the work. For heavy spreadsheet or editor navigation without layers, a 75% or TKL is the better fit.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: The 2.4GHz receiver and USB-C wired work on macOS, and VIA runs in a Chromium-based browser, so it's available on Mac too. That's an advantage over boards whose remapping utility is Windows-only.
