## FAQ

- **Q: Is this a magnetic switch or a regular mechanical switch?**
  A: Magnetic (Hall Effect). The Clear Mag switch is Epomaker's magnetic line — the actuation is read by a Hall Effect sensor rather than a metal contact, which is what enables adjustable actuation and rapid trigger.

- **Q: Does it support rapid trigger?**
  A: Epomaker's HE series spec includes adjustable actuation and rapid trigger. If rapid trigger is a hard requirement, confirm the current firmware feature set with the Build Team before purchase — they can verify against the live spec sheet at Onehunga.

- **Q: Why is it wired only?**
  A: At this tier, wired keeps the price down and removes the battery and dongle variables. If you need wireless magnetic, Epomaker's G84 HE (KEYEPOG84HWJ) is the tri-mode option in the same brand.

- **Q: Why no arrow keys? Is that a problem?**
  A: The 65% layout has no dedicated arrow cluster — arrow functions sit on the Fn layer. For gaming and general typing that's fine; for heavy spreadsheet or editor navigation, a 75% or TKL board is the better fit.

- **Q: Is the magnetic feel different from a mechanical switch?**
  A: Yes. HE switches typically need a heavier return spring to overcome the magnet-sensor interaction, so a "light" HE switch can feel heavier on the return than a comparably-rated mechanical linear. For long typing sessions that's worth knowing; for gaming the faster reset is the payoff.

- **Q: Can I use it with a Mac?**
  A: USB-C wired works on macOS out of the box. The actuation and rapid-trigger software is Windows-first; on Mac you'll use the system defaults.
