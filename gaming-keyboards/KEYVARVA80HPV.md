## FAQ

- **Q: What's the difference between the Prestige Voice and Prestige Silent?**
  A: Both are Kailh's Prestige line; the Voice is tuned for an audible character and the Silent is dampened for a quieter keystroke. Same board otherwise — this SKU is the Voice. If you game late at night in a shared house or you're often on a microphone, the Silent variant (KEYVARVA80HPS) is the safer pick.

- **Q: Is this board magnetic or mechanical?**
  A: Mechanical. The magnetic Varmilo models are the Victory 67 and Muse65. The VA80 Hygge uses the Kailh Prestige mechanical switch, which means no rapid trigger but also none of the heavier HE return-spring feel.

- **Q: Does it have arrow keys and an F-row?**
  A: Yes — the 84-key layout keeps the function row, the arrow cluster, and a function block, with the numpad dropped. That's the difference versus the 65% premium options.

- **Q: Are the switches hot-swappable?**
