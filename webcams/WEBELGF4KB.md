## FAQ

- **Q: Do I need a capture card for 4K?**
  A: No — the Facecam 4K is a USB webcam; it handles 4K60 over USB-C directly to your PC. A capture card is only needed for a separate mirrorless/DSLR camera.

- **Q: 4K vs 1080p — is 4K actually better for streaming?**
  A: For streaming, your encoder usually downscales to 1080p, but a 4K sensor still captures more detail and handles cropping/zoom better. For local recording and content creation, 4K is a clear upgrade. The bigger win is the sensor quality (Sony STARVIS) and low-light performance.

- **Q: Low light — how does it really perform?**
  A: Strong for a webcam. The Sony STARVIS sensor + HDR handles dim and mixed-light rooms well. It's not a low-light sensor for near-darkness (a dedicated cam would do better), but for typical home offices it's excellent.
