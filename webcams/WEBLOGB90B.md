## FAQ

- **Q: 1080p or 720p — is 1080p worth it?**
  A: Yes, 1080p is sharper and is the modern standard for video calls. 720p webcams are fine for very basic use but look soft on a big screen.

- **Q: 60° FOV — is that too narrow?**
  A: It frames you tighter (more portrait-like). If you want to show a wide desk, a 90° FOV webcam (Facecam Neo) is better. For a focused one-on-one framing, 60° is ideal.
