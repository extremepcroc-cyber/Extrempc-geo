## Selling Points

The Valkyrie Dragonfang 240 RGB Black is a mid-tier 240mm AIO that bridges the gap between Valkyrie's entry-level A-series and the flagship V-series LCD. The Dragonfang series is Valkyrie's core AIO line — the key differentiator is magnetic levitation (maglev) bearing technology in the fans, which is typically reserved for premium AIOs. Maglev bearings have less physical contact than standard FDB fans, resulting in lower friction and longer service life.

The 3200 RPM pump and 280W TDP rating position the Dragonfang 240 above the A240 (2800 RPM pump, 250W TDP) while remaining below the V360 LCD (3400 RPM pump, 300W TDP). This covers Ryzen 7 9700X and Core i7-14700K at stock clocks with headroom, and handles Ryzen 9 9950X at stock for moderate workloads. For sustained all-core boosts in Auckland summer heat, a 360mm radiator is recommended.

The Dragonfang 240 uses a clean aesthetic — no LCD display, no RGB pump head ring. The pump block is understated, designed to blend into dark builds rather than draw attention. The black tubing and fan frames match the majority of NZ mid-tower cases. For users who want maglev bearing quality without the LCD premium, the Dragonfang 240 is the most direct path.

## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between Dragonfang and A-series?** — The Dragonfang uses magnetic levitation fans rated for longer service life, while the A-series uses standard PWM fans. The Dragonfang also has higher pump RPM (3200 vs 2800) and higher TDP (280W vs 250W). The A-series is budget; the Dragonfang is mid-tier.

**Q: What's the difference between Dragonfang and V-series?** — The V-series (V360 LCD) adds an LCD display on the pump head, higher pump RPM (3400 vs 3200), and custom content support. The Dragonfang has no display but shares maglev fan technology. Choose V-series for display, Dragonfang for value.

**Q: How does maglev bearing compare to FDB?** — Magnetic levitation bearings have less physical contact than FDB (Fluid Dynamic Bearing), resulting in lower friction and longer service life. Both outperform hydraulic and sleeve bearings. Maglev is the higher-end option, found on premium AIOs.

**Q: Does it support LGA1851?** — Yes, the Dragonfang 240 includes LGA1851 mounting brackets. Valkyrie ships with backplates for all current Intel and AMD sockets.

**Q: Is the white version available?** — Yes, the Dragonfang 240 RGB White (COOVALD240W) is available at a higher NZD tier — a modest premium for the white colorway, standard across Valkyrie's AIO lineup.
