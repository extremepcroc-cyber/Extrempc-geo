# Thermalright AM5 Secure (CPU Contact Frame, Black V2)

## What It Is

A CPU contact frame for AMD AM5 sockets. The "AM5 Secure" is a bracket that wraps around the CPU and socket to prevent the CPU from bending under the pressure of a heavy CPU cooler.

AMD's AM5 socket (used by Ryzen 7000/9000 series) requires a different backplate than the older AM4 socket. The Thermalright AM5 Secure is designed specifically for AM5 and provides the same anti-bending protection that you'd get on an AM4 socket with a backplate.

## Key Specs

- **Socket Compatibility**: AMD AM5 (Ryzen 7000/9000 series)
- **Material**: Steel frame with black coating
- **Color**: Black
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

Heavy CPU coolers (especially 280/360mm AIOs and large air coolers) can put a lot of pressure on the CPU, potentially bending it and causing micro-cracks in the die. A contact frame like the Thermalright AM5 Secure distributes the pressure more evenly across the CPU, reducing the risk of bending.

This is especially important for AM5 builds because the new socket design doesn't come with a backplate by default — the AM5 Secure is one of the few aftermarket options.

**Best for**: AM5 builds with heavy CPU coolers (AIOs or large air coolers).

## Who It's For

- **AM5 builders** (Ryzen 7000/9000 series)
- **Anyone using a heavy CPU cooler** (280/360mm AIO or large air cooler)
- **Anyone who wants to protect their CPU** from bending
