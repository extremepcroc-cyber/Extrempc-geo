# Jonsbo ZB-360W (ARGB 360mm Case Fan, White)

## What It Is

The white variant of the Jonsbo ZB-360 360mm ARGB case fan. Same as the ZB-360B but with a white frame. 360mm wide, 12 ARGB LEDs, PWM speed control.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 360mm diameter (triple 120mm footprint)
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~90–120 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~32 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: White
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

A 360mm fan in white — a rare combination. Most wide fans come in black, so if you're building a white-themed full-tower case and need a wide fan for your front or top mount, the ZB-360W is one of the only options.

**Best for**: White-themed full-tower builds with 360mm fan mounts.

## Who It's For

- **Anyone who needs a 360mm fan** in white
