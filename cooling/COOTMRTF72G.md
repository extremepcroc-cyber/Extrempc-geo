# Thermalright TF7 Thermal Paste (2g)

## What It Is

A 2g tube of Thermalright TF7 thermal paste. The TF7 is a high-performance, non-conductive thermal paste designed for CPU and GPU cooling.

The "2g" tube is a smaller size than the 4g variant — useful for a single build or if you want to try a new paste without committing to a larger tube.

## Key Specs

- **Volume**: 2g
- **Type**: Non-conductive silicone-based paste
- **Thermal Conductivity**: ~8.5 W/mK
- **Application**: CPU, GPU, VRAM, chipset
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

Thermal paste is essential for good CPU/GPU cooling. The TF7 is a high-performance paste with good thermal conductivity — it transfers heat efficiently from the CPU to the heatsink.

The TF7 is one of the cheapest high-performance thermal pastes on the market at ~$7. It's a solid choice for a single build or if you want to try a new paste.

**Best for**: CPU/GPU cooling, or anyone who wants a high-performance thermal paste.
