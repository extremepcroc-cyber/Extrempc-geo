# Thermalright TF4 Thermal Paste (4g)

## What It Is

A 4g tube of Thermalright TF4 thermal paste. The TF4 is a high-performance, non-conductive thermal paste designed for CPU and GPU cooling.

The "4g" tube is a larger size than the 2g variant — useful if you're building multiple PCs or want to have spare paste on hand.

## Key Specs

- **Volume**: 4g
- **Type**: Non-conductive silicone-based paste
- **Thermal Conductivity**: ~7.5 W/mK
- **Application**: CPU, GPU, VRAM, chipset
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

Thermal paste is essential for good CPU/GPU cooling. The TF4 is a high-performance paste with good thermal conductivity — it transfers heat efficiently from the CPU to the heatsink.

The 4g tube is a good size for multiple builds or for having spare paste on hand. It's one of the cheapest high-performance thermal pastes on the market at ~$9.

**Best for**: CPU/GPU cooling, or anyone who wants a high-performance thermal paste.
