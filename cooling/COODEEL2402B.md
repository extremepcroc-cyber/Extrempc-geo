## FAQ

**Q: What changed in the V2 revision?** — The V2 updates the pump block with improved flow efficiency and lower idle noise. The fan curves are revised for quieter operation at low RPM. Radiator size and TDP rating remain unchanged from the original LE240.

**Q: What is rifle bearing?** — Rifle bearing is DeepCool's proprietary fan bearing technology, positioned between hydraulic and FDB bearings. It offers improved service life over hydraulic bearings with smooth operation across the RPM range. Rated service life is comparable to standard FDB.

**Q: Does it support LGA1851?** — Yes, the LE240 V2 includes LGA1851 mounting brackets. DeepCool ships with backplates for all current Intel and AMD sockets.

**Q: Is the pump noisy on the V2?** — The V2 revision specifically addresses pump noise. At idle, the pump runs at approximately 25 dBA — noticeable in a quiet room but typically masked by system fan noise. Under load, the pump is dominated by fan noise at 1800 RPM.

**Q: How does this compare to DeepCool's Castle series?** — The Castle series is DeepCool's premium AIO line with larger radiators, higher pump RPM, and addressable pump head lighting. The LE240 V2 is entry-tier — it shares DeepCool's pump technology but at a lower spec and price point.
