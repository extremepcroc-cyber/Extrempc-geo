## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between SFP and FDB bearing?** — SFP (Super Fluid Dynamic) is Jonsbo's proprietary bearing design, positioned between standard FDB and magnetic levitation. SFP uses a refined fluid film for smoother operation at higher RPM than standard FDB, with rated service life comparable to FDB.

**Q: What does semi-rigid tubing mean?** — The TF3 uses reinforced tubing that holds its shape rather than drooping like soft rubber tubing. This provides a cleaner look in showcase builds and is more resistant to kinking over the AIO's service life. The tubing is not fully rigid (metal-braided), so it still allows some flexibility for installation.

**Q: Does the TF3 have any display on the pump head?** — No. The TF3-360SCB has a clean pump block with no LCD, RGB ring, or display. The ARGB lighting is on the fans only. This is intentional — the TF3 targets users who prefer minimal aesthetics.

**Q: Is Jonsbo reliable for AIO coolers?** — Jonsbo is established in the NZ case market but newer to AIO cooling. The TF3 series represents Jonsbo's commitment to the cooling segment. The 2-year warranty through ExtremePC covers pump failure, and Jonsbo's case manufacturing track record suggests build quality.

**Q: Does it fit with LGA1851?** — Yes, the TF3 includes mounting brackets for Intel LGA1851/LGA1700/LGA1200/LGA115x and AMD AM5/AM4. Thermal paste is pre-applied.
