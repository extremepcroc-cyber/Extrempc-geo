# Jonsbo ZC-360B (ARGB 360mm Case Fan with Screen Display, Black)

## What It Is

A 360mm ARGB case fan with a built-in screen display from Jonsbo. The "ZC" series is Jonsbo's premium fan line — the "C" stands for the screen display. It's a 360mm-wide fan (spans three 120mm mounts) with 12 ARGB LEDs and a small LCD screen on the fan hub that can display system stats (CPU temp, GPU temp, FPS, etc.) or custom images.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 360mm diameter (triple 120mm footprint)
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~90–120 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~32 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Screen**: Built-in LCD display (shows system stats or custom images)
- **Frame**: Black
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The ZC-360B is Jonsbo's most premium fan. The screen display is the standout feature — it can show real-time CPU/GPU temps, FPS, or custom images. It's a statement piece for builds where you want your case to look as good as it performs.

The 360mm width means it's designed for full-tower cases with 360mm fan mounts or 360mm radiators.

**Best for**: High-end showcase builds where you want a premium fan with a screen display.
