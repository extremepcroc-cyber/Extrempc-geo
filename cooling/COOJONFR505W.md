# Jonsbo FR-505W (120mm PWM ARGB Case Fan, White)

## What It Is

The white variant of the Jonsbo FR-505 120mm slim ARGB case fan. Same as the FR-505B but with a white frame instead of black. 25mm slim profile, 12 ARGB LEDs, PWM speed control. A cheap, clean-looking fan for white-themed builds.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm (slim)
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: White
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The FR-505W is the white version of one of the cheapest 120mm ARGB case fans on the market. At ~$20, it's hard to beat for a white-themed build where you want clean fans with RGB lighting. The 25mm slim profile fits in most standard case mounts without clearance issues.

**Best for**: White-build enthusiasts who want cheap ARGB case fans.

## Who It's For
