# Thermalright TF8 Thermal Paste (2g)

## What It Is

A 2g tube of Thermalright TF8 thermal paste. The TF8 is a high-performance, non-conductive thermal paste designed for CPU and GPU cooling.

The TF8 is the top-tier paste in Thermalright's TF series — it has the highest thermal conductivity of the three (TF4, TF7, TF8), making it the best choice for overclocking and maximum cooling performance.
