**Mid-tier positioning in the Segotep AIO lineup**: The BeAced 360 sits above the BeVere entry series and below the IceMoon higher-tier model. It bridges the gap between budget and performance-oriented AIOs, offering refined ARGB fan frames and an upgraded pump block over the BeVere. The micro-SD pump reduces vibration transfer to the cold plate, resulting in lower audible noise during sustained loads compared to the BeVere's standard pump.

**Triple-fan 360mm radiator for NZ summer thermals**: Auckland summers regularly push case temperatures above 30 degrees Celsius with high humidity. The 360mm radiator surface area provides significantly more dissipation capacity than a 240mm variant, keeping CPU boost clocks stable during extended gaming sessions or render workloads. Three 120mm PWM fans scale airflow from idle silence to full-speed when the pump signals thermal headroom is needed.

**ARGB synchronization across all fans**: The 5V 3-pin ARGB header connects to any modern motherboard's RGB control, enabling synchronized lighting with case fans, memory modules, and GPU LEDs. Segotep's ARGB implementation uses addressable LEDs per fan rather than a single-zone strip, giving per-fan color control instead of uniform strips.

**Copper cold plate with direct-contact design**: The copper base makes direct contact with the CPU IHS, maximizing thermal transfer. Unlike aluminum cold plates found on entry-tier AIOs, copper's higher thermal conductivity (398 W/mK vs 205 W/mK) means more efficient heat extraction at the source.
