## What It Is

The reverse-rotation variant of the Jonsbo ZB-360. Same as the ZB-360B but the fan spins in the opposite direction — useful for push/pull radiator setups.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 360mm diameter (triple 120mm footprint)
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: Black
- **Rotation**: Reverse
- **Warranty**: Manufacturer warranty, claims handled through ExtremePC NZ (term set by the manufacturer; see the product page)

## Why It Matters

The reverse-rotation fan is the counterpart to the standard (positive) ZB-360B. In a push/pull 360mm radiator setup, you'd want one standard and one reverse so both fans push air through the radiator in the same direction.

**Best for**: Push/pull 360mm radiator setups.
