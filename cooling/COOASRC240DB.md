This is valuable for NZ home offices and bedrooms where the PC runs overnight tasks (Windows Update, backups, downloads) and silence matters more than active cooling. ## FAQ

**Q: What does the LCD display show?** — The 3-inch segment LED display shows CPU temperature, pump RPM, and fan speed in real-time. The content is fixed to these three readouts — it does not support custom images, animations, or G-code like the ASRock Steel Legend display. The 480 x 480 resolution provides crisp, legible numbers.

**Q: Does the 0dB fan mode work on all motherboards?** — The 0dB Silent Cooling feature requires the motherboard to support fan stop mode (most modern ASUS, MSI, Gigabyte, and ASRock boards do). Set the minimum PWM speed to 0% in your BIOS fan curve. If your motherboard does not support it, fans will run at the minimum configured speed.

**Q: Is the USB 9-pin connector for the display easy to find?** — The USB 9-pin header is a standard internal USB 2.0 connector found on all modern motherboards. Label it as USB_INT or JUSB. The ASRock manual identifies the exact header location for popular motherboard models.

**Q: 3200 RPM pump — is that loud?** — The pump operates within a 1200–3200 RPM range, scaling with load. At idle, it runs near 1200 RPM which is inaudible. Under full load at 3200 RPM, the pump is rated below the 31 dB(A) fan maximum — the fans are the dominant noise source, not the pump.

**Q: Can I use this with LGA1851?** — Yes, the Challenger 240 Digital ships with mounting brackets for LGA1851, LGA1700, AM5, and AM4. ASRock includes all backplates in the box.
