# Segotep Irisflo U120F (120mm ARGB Reverse Case Fan, White)

## What It Is

The white reverse-rotation variant of the Segotep Irisflo U120. Same as the U120F but with a white frame. 12 ARGB LEDs, PWM speed control, reverse rotation.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: White
- **Rotation**: Reverse
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

A white reverse-rotation ARGB fan at ~$15 — one of the cheapest options for a push/pull AIO setup in a white-themed build.

**Best for**: Push/pull AIO setups in white-themed builds.

## Who It's For

- **White-build AIO enthusiasts** doing push/pull
- **Budget builders** who want a cheap white reverse-rotation ARGB fan
