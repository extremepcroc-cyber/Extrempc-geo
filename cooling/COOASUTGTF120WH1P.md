## Why It Matters

That makes it a solid choice for case ventilation, especially if you're building a case with 3–4 fan mounts and want decent airflow without paying for premium fans.

The white frame is a big plus for white-themed builds. If you're building a white-themed PC and need one or two case fans, this is a good option.
