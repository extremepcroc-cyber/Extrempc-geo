# ASUS TUF Gaming TF120 ARGB (1-Pack, White)

## What It Is

A single 120mm PWM case fan with ARGB lighting from ASUS's TUF Gaming line. The white frame makes it a clean match for white-themed builds. Each fan has 12 ARGB LEDs, a 4-pin PWM connector for speed control, and a 3-pin ARGB connector for lighting sync. It's a simple, no-frills case fan — not a fan hub, not a smart fan, just a good-quality 120mm fan with RGB.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: up to 51.8 CFM
- **Static Pressure**: up to 2.5 mmH₂O
- **Noise**: 28.9 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: White
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The TF120 ARGB is one of the higher-airflow 120mm fans in its price bracket — 51.8 CFM is more than you'd expect from a $23 fan. That makes it a solid choice for case ventilation, especially if you're building a case with 3–4 fan mounts and want decent airflow without paying for premium fans.

The white frame is a big plus for white-themed builds. It's not a "pure white" fan (the frame is white, but the hub and some internal parts are black), but it's clean enough to look good in a white build. The ARGB lighting syncs with ASUS Aura Sync, MSI Mystic Light, Gigabyte RGB Fusion, and ASRock Polychrome.

**Best for**: Budget builders who want a white 120mm case fan with RGB and decent airflow. If you're building a white-themed PC and need one or two case fans, this is a good option.
