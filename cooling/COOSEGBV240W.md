The Segotep BeVere 240 ARGB White is an entry-tier white 240mm AIO. The BeVere series is Segotep's entry AIO line — below the IceMoon and MU series. Segotep's AIO lineup is newer to the NZ market compared to Thermalright or ASRock, but the BeVere competes on pricing.

The 240mm form factor is the most versatile radiator size for mid-tower builds. Front-mounting a 240mm AIO leaves the top position open for a case exhaust fan, improving internal airflow. In NZ summer conditions, the combination of front-intake AIO and top exhaust is more effective than a top-mounted AIO alone.

## FAQ

**Q: 250W TDP — is that enough for my CPU?** — 250W covers Ryzen 5 7600X (65W-105W boost) and Ryzen 7 9700X (105W boost) at stock clocks with margin. For Ryzen 9 9950X or Core i9-14900K, a higher-TDP-rated AIO is recommended.

**Q: Does it support LGA1851?** — Yes, the BeVere 240 White includes LGA1851 mounting brackets. Segotep ships with backplates for all current Intel and AMD sockets.
