# Antec P12 PWM ARGB (3-Pack)

## What It Is

A 3-pack of 120mm PWM case fans with ARGB lighting, designed for building a fully lit-up PC without buying three separate fans. Each fan runs on a 4-pin PWM header for speed control and has 12 ARGB LEDs for colourful lighting. The 3-pack is daisy-chained so you only need one fan header and one ARGB header on your motherboard — clean cable management, no fan-hub needed.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm (standard thickness)
- **Bearing**: Hydro Dynamic Bearing (HDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 300–1,400 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: up to 42.5 CFM per fan
- **Static Pressure**: up to 2.48 mmH₂O
- **Noise**: 18.3 dBA (max, single fan)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB per fan, 36 total
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V, 4-pin connector)
- **Cable Length**: daisy-chained, ~450mm total
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

If you're building a new PC and want a full set of case fans without the hassle of buying three separate fans and figuring out how to connect them, a 3-pack is the way to go. The P12 PWM ARGB is one of the best-value 3-packs on the market — you get 42.5 CFM of airflow per fan, which is more than enough for a standard ATX or mATX case, and the ARGB lighting syncs with major RGB ecosystems (ASUS Aura Sync, MSI Mystic Light, Gigabyte RGB Fusion, ASRock Polychrome).

The 300 RPM minimum speed means you can run the fans at very low speeds for near-silent operation while gaming, and spin them up to 1,400 RPM when you need maximum airflow. The daisy-chain design means you only need one fan header and one ARGB header — no fan hub, no extra cables.

**Best for**: Budget builders who want a full set of case fans with RGB lighting in one purchase. If you're building a case with 3–4 fan mounts (2 front + 1 rear, or 3 front + 1 rear), this 3-pack covers the bulk of it.
