# Segotep HQ-12 Pro MEOW (120mm Case Fan, Orange)

## What It Is

A 120mm case fan from Segotep with a playful orange "MEOW" theme. The HQ-12 Pro is Segotep's mid-range 120mm fan lineup, and the MEOW variant is a fun, colorful edition with an orange frame and matching orange ARGB LEDs. It's a budget-friendly fan with ARGB lighting and PWM speed control.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: Orange (MEOW theme)
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The HQ-12 Pro MEOW is a fun, colorful fan for builders who want a bit of personality in their case. The orange frame and matching ARGB LEDs make it a standout in an otherwise black or white build.

At ~$20, it's one of the cheapest 120mm ARGB fans on the market. It's not a high-performance fan, but it's quiet, it looks fun, and it's cheap enough to fill a case without breaking the bank.

**Best for**: Color-themed builds or anyone who wants a fun, affordable ARGB case fan.
