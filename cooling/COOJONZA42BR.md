## What It Is

The reverse-rotation variant of the Jonsbo ZA-420 420mm ARGB case fan. Same as the ZA-420B but the fan spins in the opposite direction — useful for push/pull radiator setups where you need one fan pushing air in and one pulling air out.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 420mm diameter (14-inch)
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: Black
- **Rotation**: Reverse
- **Warranty**: Manufacturer warranty, claims handled through ExtremePC NZ (term set by the manufacturer; see the product page)

## Why It Matters

The reverse-rotation fan is the counterpart to the standard (positive) ZA-420B. In a push/pull 420mm radiator setup, you'd want one standard and one reverse so both fans push air through the radiator in the same direction.

**Best for**: Push/pull 420mm radiator setups.
