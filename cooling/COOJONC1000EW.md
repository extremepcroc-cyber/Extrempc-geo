# Jonsbo CR-1000 EVO ARGB (White)

## What It Is

A single-tower CPU air cooler with ARGB lighting from Jonsbo, one of the most popular budget cooler brands in the NZ market. The CR-1000 EVO is a 120mm single-tower design — one 120mm fan pushing air through a single heatsink tower. It's designed for standard ATX/mATX builds where you don't need the height of a dual-tower cooler but want better cooling than a stock Intel cooler.

## Key Specs

- **Type**: Single-tower air cooler
- **Heatsink Height**: ~145mm (fits most ATX/mATX cases)
- **Fan**: 1× 120mm ARGB PWM (included)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM
- **Airflow**: ~45 CFM (fan)
- **TDP Rating**: 95W (sufficient for most non-K CPUs, adequate for K CPUs at stock)
- **Heat Pipes**: 4× 6mm direct-contact heat pipes
- **Mounting**: Intel LGA 1700/1200/115x, AMD AM4/AM5
- **ARGB**: 3-pin (5V) header
- **Frame**: White
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The CR-1000 EVO is one of the best-value CPU coolers in its price bracket. At $44, it's significantly cheaper than a Noctua NH-U12S or a Deepcool AK400, but it delivers comparable real-world performance for 65–95W CPUs. The white frame is a big plus for white-themed builds, and the ARGB fan adds a touch of colour without looking over the top.

The single-tower design means it's shorter and lighter than dual-tower coolers, so it fits in more cases and doesn't require a heavy backplate. The 4 heat pipes provide good heat transfer, and the 120mm fan is quiet at low RPMs.

**Best for**: Budget builders who want a better CPU cooler than stock but don't want to pay $100+. If you're running an i5-13400, i5-14400, Ryzen 5 7600, or similar 65–95W CPU, this cooler will keep it cool and quiet.
