## FAQ

**Q: What does the LCD display show?** — Out of the box, the LCD shows real-time CPU temperature and pump RPM. You can upload custom images, GIFs, or static content via Thermalright's configuration software. The screen is large enough to read from a seated position with a tempered-glass case.

**Q: Is the LCD bright enough to see in a lit room?** — The LCD uses a standard IPS panel with sufficient brightness for typical office or bedroom environments. In direct sunlight it may appear dim, but inside a case with ambient room lighting it is clearly visible.

**Q: Does it support LGA1851?** — Yes. The Stream Vision ships with mounting brackets for LGA1851, LGA1700, LGA1200, LGA115x, AM5, and AM4. Thermalright includes a universal backplate compatible with all listed sockets.

**Q: How does the Stream Vision compare to the Trofeo Vision thermally?** — Both use the same 360mm radiator and copper cold plate construction.
