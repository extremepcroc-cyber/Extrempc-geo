## Selling Points

The Valkyrie A240 ARGB is an entry-tier 240mm AIO at $129 that competes directly with ASRock's Pro series and Thermalright's Aqua Elite line. Valkyrie is a Korean brand known for higher-end AIOs (V360 LCD, Dragonfang series), and the A240 brings that brand's pump technology into the budget segment. Dual 120mm ARGB PWM fans with standard PWM control cover the 500-1800 RPM range.

At $129, the A240 sits between the budget Thermalright Aqua Elite 240 ($99) and the ASRock Pro 240 ($119) on pricing, but Valkyrie's reputation in the enthusiast AIO segment adds brand credibility that the cheaper alternatives lack. The 2800 RPM pump handles 250W TDP — adequate for Ryzen 5 7600X through Ryzen 7 9700X at stock clocks.

The 240mm form factor is the most versatile radiator size for mid-tower builds. Front-mounting a 240mm AIO leaves the top position open for a case exhaust fan, improving internal airflow. In NZ summer conditions, the combination of front-intake AIO and top exhaust is more effective than a top-mounted AIO alone.

## FAQ

**Q: How does the A240 compare to Valkyrie's premium models?** — The A240 shares Valkyrie's pump design language but uses standard PWM fans rather than the magnetic levitation bearings found on the V360 LCD and Dragonfang series. The A240 is entry-tier; the premium models add bearing quality, LCD displays, and higher pump RPM.

**Q: Does it come with thermal paste?** — Yes, thermal paste is pre-applied on the cold plate. No additional paste needed for standard installation.

**Q: Is the ARGB 5V compatible?** — Yes, the A240 uses standard 5V 3-pin ARGB connectors that sync with ASUS Aura, MSI Mystic Light, Gigabyte RGB Fusion, and ASRock Polychrome Sync motherboard software.

**Q: What's the maximum CPU this can handle?** — The 250W TDP rating covers Ryzen 7 9700X and Core i7-14700K at stock clocks. For Ryzen 9 9950X or Core i9-14900K, a 360mm radiator is recommended for sustained thermal headroom.

**Q: White version available?** — Yes, the Valkyrie A240 White (COOVALA240W) is available at $139 — a $10 premium for the white colorway, standard across the AIO market.
