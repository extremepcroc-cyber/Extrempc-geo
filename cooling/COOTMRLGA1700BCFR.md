## What It Is

A CPU contact frame (anti-bending buckle) for Intel LGA 1700 sockets. The red retrofit kit is a bracket that wraps around the CPU and socket to prevent the CPU from bending under the pressure of a heavy CPU cooler.

LGA 1700 is the socket used by Intel's 12th/13th/14th gen Core processors. The contact frame is especially important for builds with heavy coolers (AIOs or large air coolers).

## Why It Matters

Heavy CPU coolers can put a lot of pressure on the CPU, potentially bending it and causing micro-cracks in the die. A contact frame like the Thermalright LGA 1700 BCF distributes the pressure more evenly across the CPU, reducing the risk of bending.

The red color is a fun accent — if you're building a red-themed build and want a CPU cooler with a red accent, the LGA 1700 BCF (red) is a good match.

**Best for**: LGA 1700 builds with heavy CPU coolers.
