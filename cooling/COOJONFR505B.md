# Jonsbo FR-505B (120mm PWM ARGB Case Fan, Black)

## What It Is

A 25mm-thin 120mm PWM case fan with ARGB lighting from Jonsbo. The FR-505 is part of Jonsbo's budget fan lineup — slim profile, standard 120mm diameter, and 12 ARGB LEDs for lighting. The "B" variant is black. The 25mm thickness makes it a good fit for cases with tight fan clearance, and the PWM connector means you can control fan speed from your motherboard.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm (slim)
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: Black
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The FR-505 is one of the cheapest 120mm ARGB case fans on the NZ market at ~$20. It's not a high-performance fan — you won't get the airflow of a Noctua or a Phanteks — but it's quiet, it looks good with ARGB lighting, and it's cheap enough to fill a case without breaking the bank.

The 25mm slim profile is a practical plus. Many cases have tight clearance between the fan mount and the front panel or radiator, and a standard 25mm fan (like a Noctua NF-A12x25) won't fit in some of those spaces. The FR-505 is thin enough to squeeze into most standard mounts.

**Best for**: Budget builders who want ARGB case fans without paying $30+ per fan. If you're filling a case with 4–6 fans and want something cheap with RGB, this is the way to go.
