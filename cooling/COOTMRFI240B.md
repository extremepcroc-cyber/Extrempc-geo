## FAQ

**Q: What is FDB bearing and why does it matter?** — FDB (Fluid Dynamic Bearing) fans use a fluid film for bearing support, reducing friction and wear. FDB fans are rated for 150,000 hours vs ~40,000 hours for sleeve bearings. At 24/7 operation, that's roughly 17 years vs 4.5 years. FDB also runs quieter at higher RPM, which matters during sustained load in NZ summer.

**Q: 250W TDP — is that enough for my CPU?** — 250W covers Ryzen 5 7600X (65W-105W boost) and Ryzen 7 9700X (105W boost) at stock clocks with margin. For Ryzen 9 9950X or Core i9-14900K, a higher-TDP-rated AIO is recommended.

**Q: Does it support LGA1851?** — Yes, the Frozen Infinity 240 includes LGA1851 mounting brackets. Thermalright ships with backplates for all current Intel and AMD sockets.

**Q: How long is the warranty?** — Thermalright provides 3-year manufacturer warranty through ExtremePC NZ — the longest standard warranty in the mid-tier AIO segment.

**Q: Is the 240mm enough or should I get 360mm?** — The 240mm is adequate for mid-range CPUs at stock clocks. If your case supports 360mm and you have a high-TDP CPU, the extra radiator area provides 3-5°C thermal headroom. For Ryzen 7 or below, the 240mm is sufficient.
