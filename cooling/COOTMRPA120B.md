# Thermalright Peerless Assassin 120 (Black CPU Cooler)

## What It Is

A dual-tower CPU air cooler from Thermalright. The Peerless Assassin 120 (PA120) is one of the best-performing budget CPU air coolers on the market — it's a dual-tower design with 6 heatpipes and 2 120mm fans.

The PA120 is Thermalright's flagship air cooler — it cools as well as many AIOs at a fraction of the price. It's a popular choice for high-end builds where you want good cooling without the cost of an AIO.

## Key Specs

- **Type**: Dual-tower CPU air cooler
- **Heatpipes**: 6× 6mm (direct contact)
- **Fans**: 2× 120mm (PWM)
- **Height**: ~155mm (with fans)
- **Socket Support**: AMD AM4/AM5, Intel 115x/1200/1700/1851
- **LEDs**: None (non-ARGB)
- **Color**: Black
- **Warranty**: 3 years
