# Segotep Meta 12F (120mm Reverse Case Fan, White)

## What It Is

The white reverse-rotation variant of the Segotep Meta 12. Same as the Meta 12F (black) but with a white frame. No RGB, no ARGB — just a functional fan with PWM speed control.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: None (non-ARGB)
- **Frame**: White
- **Rotation**: Reverse
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

A white reverse-rotation fan at ~$17 — one of the cheapest options for a push/pull AIO setup in a white-themed build. No RGB, but functional and affordable.

**Best for**: Push/pull AIO setups in white-themed builds on a budget.

## Who It's For

- **White-build AIO enthusiasts** doing push/pull
- **Budget builders** who want a cheap white reverse-rotation fan
