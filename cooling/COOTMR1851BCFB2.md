## What It Is

A CPU contact frame buckle for Intel LGA 1851 sockets. The "BCF" stands for "Backplate Contact Frame" — it's a bracket that wraps around the CPU and socket to prevent the CPU from bending under the pressure of a heavy CPU cooler.

Intel's 1851 socket (used by Arrow Lake processors) is a new socket design that requires a different backplate than the older 1700 socket. The Thermalright BCF is designed specifically for 1851 and provides the same anti-bending protection that you'd get on a 1700 socket with a backplate.

## Why It Matters

Heavy CPU coolers (especially 280/360mm AIOs and large air coolers) can put a lot of pressure on the CPU, potentially bending it and causing micro-cracks in the die. A contact frame buckle like the Thermalright BCF distributes the pressure more evenly across the CPU, reducing the risk of bending.

This is especially important for 1851-socket builds because the new socket design doesn't come with a backplate by default — the BCF is one of the few aftermarket options.

**Best for**: LGA 1851 builds with heavy CPU coolers (AIOs or large air coolers).
