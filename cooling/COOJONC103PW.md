# Jonsbo CR-1000 V3 PRO ARGB (White)

## What It Is

The next step up from the CR-1000 EVO — Jonsbo's CR-1000 V3 PRO is a single-tower CPU air cooler with a redesigned heatsink and ARGB fan. The "PRO" in the name refers to improved thermal performance over the EVO, with a larger heatsink and a higher-airflow fan. It's still a single-tower design, so it fits in more cases than dual-tower coolers, but it moves more air.

## Key Specs

- **Type**: Single-tower air cooler
- **Heatsink Height**: ~150mm
- **Fan**: 1× 120mm ARGB PWM (included)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,200 RPM
- **Airflow**: ~50 CFM (fan)
- **TDP Rating**: 105W (handles K-series CPUs at stock)
- **Heat Pipes**: 4× 6mm direct-contact heat pipes
- **Mounting**: Intel LGA 1700/1200/115x, AMD AM4/AM5
- **ARGB**: 3-pin (5V) header
- **Frame**: White
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The CR-1000 V3 PRO is a sweet-spot cooler for people who want better performance than the EVO but don't want to spend $80+ on a dual-tower cooler. At $58, it's still in budget territory, but the 50 CFM fan and larger heatsink give it a meaningful thermal advantage over the EVO.

The 2,200 RPM max fan speed (vs. 2,000 on the EVO) means it can spin up faster under load, and the 105W TDP rating means it can handle an i5-13600K or Ryzen 5 7600X at stock without thermal throttling. The white frame and ARGB fan make it a clean match for white-themed builds.

**Best for**: Builders who want a solid single-tower cooler with ARGB lighting. If you're running a mid-range K-series CPU and want better cooling than the EVO but don't want to spend $80+ on a dual-tower, this is the one.
