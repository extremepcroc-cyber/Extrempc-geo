## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between the 360mm and 240mm Dragonfang?** — The 360mm version adds a third fan and 50% more radiator surface area. The pump RPM and TDP rating are identical (3200 RPM, 280W), but the extra radiator volume provides more thermal headroom for sustained high-TDP workloads. Choose 360mm if your case supports it.

**Q: How does maglev bearing compare to FDB?** — Magnetic levitation bearings have less physical contact than FDB (Fluid Dynamic Bearing), resulting in lower friction and longer service life. Both outperform hydraulic and sleeve bearings. Maglev is the higher-end option, found on premium AIOs.

**Q: Does it support LGA1851?** — Yes, the Dragonfang 360 includes LGA1851 mounting brackets. Valkyrie ships with backplates for all current Intel and AMD sockets.

**Q: Is the cooling performance identical to the black version?** — Yes, the white Dragonfang 360 shares the same pump, fans, radiator, and cold plate as the black version. The only differences are cosmetic — white tubing, white fan frames, and white pump block housing.

**Q: Can this handle a Core i9-14900K at stock?** — The 280W TDP rating covers Core i9-14900K at stock clocks. For aggressive manual overclocking or sustained all-core rendering, the V360 LCD (300W TDP, 3400 RPM pump) provides additional headroom.
