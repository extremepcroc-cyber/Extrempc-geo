# Thermalright Valor Odin Thermal Pad (95x50x2.0mm)

## What It Is

A 95mm × 50mm × 2.0mm thermal pad from Thermalright's Valor Odin series. This is a thick, high-performance thermal pad designed for VRAM, chipset, and other heat-generating components on your motherboard.

## Key Specs

- **Size**: 95mm × 50mm × 2.0mm
- **Thermal Conductivity**: ~8 W/mK (estimated)
- **Material**: Silicone-based with ceramic filler
- **Color**: Grey
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The 2.0mm thickness makes this pad ideal for applications where a thicker pad is needed — for example, if your VRAM or chipset sits farther from the heatsink. The Valor Odin is a premium pad with better thermal conductivity than standard pads.

**Best for**: Applications where you need a thick thermal pad.

## Who It's For

- **Anyone who needs a thick thermal pad** for VRAM or chipset
- **Custom loop builders** with thicker clearances
- **Upgraders** replacing old thermal pads
