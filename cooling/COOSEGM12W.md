# Segotep Meta 12 (120mm Case Fan, White)

## What It Is

The white variant of the Segotep Meta 12. Same as the Meta 12 (black) but with a white frame. No RGB, no ARGB — just a functional fan with PWM speed control.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: None (non-ARGB)
- **Frame**: White
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

A white non-ARGB case fan at ~$17 — one of the cheapest options for a white-themed build where you just want a functional fan without RGB.

**Best for**: White-build enthusiasts who want a clean look without RGB.

## Who It's For
