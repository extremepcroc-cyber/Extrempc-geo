## FAQ

**Q: Does 360mm radiator fit my case?** — 360mm radiators require front-mount clearance in most full-tower and larger mid-tower cases. Check your case spec for "360mm radiator support" — Segotep Endura Pro+ and Radiant support front 360mm. Smaller M-ATX cases typically support only 240mm.

**Q: How does FDB bearing compare to sleeve bearing?** — FDB (Fluid Dynamic Bearing) fans are rated for 150,000 hours vs ~40,000 hours for sleeve bearings. At 24/7 operation, that's roughly 17 years vs 4.5 years. FDB also runs quieter at higher RPM, which matters during sustained load in NZ summer.

**Q: Is the pump noisy?** — The Frozen Warframe Pro pump runs at approximately 27 dBA, slightly above the Aqua Elite but within normal range for a 3200 RPM pump. At idle, with fans at 500 RPM, the combined noise is minimal — the pump is the dominant sound source at rest.

**Q: Does it support LGA1851?** — Yes, the Frozen Warframe Pro includes Intel LGA1851 mounting brackets. Thermalright ships with backplates for LGA1851/LGA1700/LGA1200/LGA115x and AMD AM5/AM4.

**Q: What happens if I only need 240mm?** — If your case supports only 240mm, consider the Thermalright Frozen Infinity 240 Black (COOTMRFI240B) at $129 — same FDB fan technology in a smaller form factor.
