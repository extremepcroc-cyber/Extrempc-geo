# Thermalright Extreme Odyssey II Thermal Pad (120x120x1.0mm)

## What It Is

A 120mm × 120mm × 1.0mm thermal pad from Thermalright's Extreme Odyssey II series. This is a high-performance thermal pad designed for VRAM, chipset, and other heat-generating components on your motherboard.

The Extreme Odyssey II series is Thermalright's premium thermal pad line — it's thicker and has better thermal conductivity than standard pads.

## Key Specs

- **Size**: 120mm × 120mm × 1.0mm
- **Thermal Conductivity**: ~8 W/mK (estimated)
- **Material**: Silicone-based with ceramic filler
- **Color**: Grey
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

Thermal pads are used to fill the gap between a heatsink and a heat-generating component (VRAM, chipset, etc.). The Extreme Odyssey II is a premium pad with better thermal conductivity than standard pads — it transfers heat more efficiently from the component to the heatsink.

This is especially useful for custom loop builds where you need to cover large areas of VRAM, or for any build where you want to improve thermal performance on your VRAM and chipset.

**Best for**: Custom loop builds, or anyone who wants to improve VRAM/chipset cooling.

## Who It's For

- **Custom loop builders** who need to cover VRAM
- **Anyone who wants to improve thermal performance** on VRAM/chipset
- **Upgraders** replacing old thermal pads with something better
