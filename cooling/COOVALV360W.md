## FAQ

**Q: What content can the LCD display?** — The pump head LCD shows CPU temperature, pump RPM, fan speed, custom static images, and animated GIF content. Content is managed through Valkyrie's companion software via the USB-C connection to your motherboard.

**Q: Does the LCD stay on at idle?** — Yes, the LCD remains active at idle. You can configure it to show a static image, temperature readout, or animated content regardless of load. The LCD power draw is minimal.

**Q: Does it need an extra USB connection?** — Yes, the V360 LCD requires a USB-C header on the motherboard for LCD content updates and pump monitoring. Most modern motherboards include at least one internal USB-C or USB 3.0 header.

**Q: How does maglev bearing compare to FDB?** — Magnetic levitation bearings have less physical contact than FDB (Fluid Dynamic Bearing), resulting in lower friction and longer service life. Both outperform hydraulic and sleeve bearings. Maglev is the higher-end option, found on premium AIOs.

**Q: Is the white version cosmetically different from the black?** — The white V360 LCD features white tubing, white fan frames, and a white pump block housing. Cooling performance and LCD functionality are identical to the black version.
