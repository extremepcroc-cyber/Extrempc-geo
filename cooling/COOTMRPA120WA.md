# Thermalright Peerless Assassin 120 (White ARGB CPU Cooler)

## What It Is

The white ARGB variant of the Thermalright Peerless Assassin 120. Same as the PA120 (black) but with a white heatsink and 2 ARGB fans.

## Key Specs

- **Type**: Dual-tower CPU air cooler
- **Heatpipes**: 6× 6mm (direct contact)
- **Fans**: 2× 120mm ARGB (PWM)
- **Height**: ~155mm (with fans)
- **Socket Support**: AMD AM4/AM5, Intel 115x/1200/1700/1851
- **LEDs**: 24 ARGB (12 per fan)
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Color**: White
- **Warranty**: 3 years
