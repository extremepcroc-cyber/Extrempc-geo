# Intel Original CPU Fan (LGA 1700 OEM)

## What It Is

The stock CPU cooler that Intel includes with their boxed processors. This is the LGA 1700 variant (12th/13th/14th Gen Intel Core). It's a basic 92mm fan with a plastic heatsink, designed to keep an unlocked 65W CPU at acceptable temperatures. It's not a performance cooler — it's a "good enough" cooler that ships in the box.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 92mm fan, ~150mm heatsink height
- **Bearing**: Ball bearing
- **Fan Speed**: ~2,300 RPM (typical)
- **Airflow**: ~25 CFM (estimated)
- **TDP Rating**: 65W
- **Mounting**: LGA 1700 (12th/13th/14th Gen Intel)
- **Noise**: ~27 dBA (typical)
- **Warranty**: 1 year (OEM)

## Why It Matters

If you bought a boxed Intel CPU (e.g., i5-13400, i5-14400, i3-13100), this cooler came in the box. You don't need to buy it separately — it's already included. This listing is for people who lost the stock cooler or need a spare.

**Do not use this cooler for:**
- Unlocked CPUs (i5-13600K, i7-13700K, i9-13900K, etc.) — these run too hot
- Overclocked CPUs — the 65W TDP rating won't keep up
- Heavy multi-core workloads — it will thermally throttle

**Fine for:**
- Base-clock, non-overclocked 65W CPUs (i3, i5 non-K, i7 non-K at stock settings)
- Light office use, browsing, 1080p gaming on integrated graphics

## Who It's For

- **People who lost their stock cooler** and need a cheap replacement
- **OEM system builders** who need a basic CPU cooler
- **Anyone running a non-K Intel CPU** at stock settings and wants a quiet, cheap cooler
