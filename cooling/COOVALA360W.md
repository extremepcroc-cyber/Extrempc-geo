## FAQ

**Q: 250W TDP — is that enough for my CPU?** — 250W covers Ryzen 5 7600X (65W-105W boost) and Ryzen 7 9700X (105W boost) at stock clocks with margin. For Ryzen 9 9950X or Core i9-14900K, a higher-TDP-rated AIO is recommended.

**Q: Does it support LGA1851?** — Yes, the A360 includes LGA1851 mounting brackets. Valkyrie ships with backplates for all current Intel and AMD sockets.

**Q: How does the A-series compare to Valkyrie's Dragonfang series?** — The Dragonfang series (COOVALD360B at $249) uses magnetic levitation fans and higher pump RPM (3200 vs 2800). The A-series is budget; the Dragonfang is mid-premium. Choose Dragonfang for bearing quality and cooling headroom.

**Q: Is the ARGB 5V compatible?** — Yes, the A360 uses standard 5V 3-pin ARGB connectors that sync with ASUS Aura, MSI Mystic Light, Gigabyte RGB Fusion, and ASRock Polychrome Sync motherboard software.

**Q: Does the white version carry a premium?** — The A360 White ($139) is priced $10 above the A240 Black ($129). The $10 premium covers both the 360mm upgrade and the white colorway.
