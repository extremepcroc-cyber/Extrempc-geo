## FAQ

**Q: Do I need a special case for 140mm fans?** — Yes. The Wonder Vision 360 Turbo requires a case with triple 140mm fan support, typically at the front or top. Not all mid-tower cases support 140mm mounts. Full-tower cases like the Lian Li PC-X57, Fractal Design Torrent, and NZXT H9 provide 140mm support. Check your case specifications before purchasing.

**Q: Are 140mm fans quieter than 120mm fans at equivalent cooling?** — Generally yes. Larger fans move more air per revolution, so they can spin slower to achieve the same airflow as smaller fans. The turbo design of the Wonder Vision's fans is optimized for high static pressure through radiator fins, which can produce a different noise profile than standard 120mm fans. Under load, the thermal advantage is measurable.

**Q: Does the Wonder Vision have an LCD display?** — No. The Wonder Vision Turbo focuses on maximum cooling through 140mm turbo fans rather than an LCD display. If you want both LCD and high-end cooling, the Stream Vision series (COOTMRSV360B/W) offers the LCD at the premium tier with 120mm fans.

**Q: Is the flagship tier worth it over the premium Stream Vision?** — If your case supports 140mm fans and you're running a high-TDP CPU (Core i9, Ryzen 9), the thermal advantage of 140mm turbo fans is meaningful, particularly in NZ summer conditions. If you're running a mid-range CPU or your case only supports 120mm, the Stream Vision or Trofeo Vision delivers sufficient cooling at a lower tier.

**Q: What's the warranty coverage?** — Thermalright provides a 3-year manufacturer warranty through ExtremePC NZ. The warranty covers pump failure, fan defects, ARGB malfunction, and manufacturing defects. RMA processing is handled locally at the Onehunga warehouse.
