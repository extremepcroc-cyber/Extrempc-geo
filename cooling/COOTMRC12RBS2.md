# Thermalright TL-C12RB-S V2 (ARGB Reverse Case Fan, Black)

## What It Is

A 120mm ARGB reverse-rotation case fan from Thermalright. The TL-C12RB-S V2 is the reverse-rotation variant of the TL-C12S — same as the TL-C12S but the fan spins in the opposite direction. Useful for push/pull radiator setups.

12 ARGB LEDs, PWM speed control, black frame.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: Black
- **Rotation**: Reverse
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

A reverse-rotation ARGB case fan at ~$15 — one of the cheapest options for a push/pull AIO setup. The TL-C12RB-S V2 is a solid budget choice if you want ARGB lighting in a push/pull setup.

**Best for**: Push/pull AIO setups on a budget.

## Who It's For

- **AIO builders** doing push/pull radiator setups
- **Budget builders** who want a cheap reverse-rotation ARGB fan
- **Anyone who wants a functional ARGB fan** in reverse configuration
