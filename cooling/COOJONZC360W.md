# Jonsbo ZC-360W (ARGB 360mm Case Fan with Screen Display, White)

## What It Is

The white variant of the Jonsbo ZC-360 — a 360mm ARGB case fan with a built-in screen display. Same as the ZC-360B but with a white frame. 12 ARGB LEDs, PWM speed control, and an LCD screen on the fan hub that can display system stats (CPU temp, GPU temp, FPS, etc.) or custom images.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 360mm diameter (triple 120mm footprint)
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~90–120 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~32 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Screen**: Built-in LCD display (shows system stats or custom images)
- **Frame**: White
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

A 360mm fan with a screen display in white — a rare combination. The screen can show real-time CPU/GPU temps, FPS, or custom images. It's a statement piece for white-themed showcase builds.

**Best for**: White-themed showcase builds where you want a premium fan with a screen display.
