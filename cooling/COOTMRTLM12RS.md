## What It Is

A 120mm ARGB reverse-rotation case fan from Thermalright. The TL-M12R-S is the reverse-rotation variant of the TL-M12-S — same as the TL-M12-S but the fan spins in the opposite direction. Useful for push/pull radiator setups.

12 ARGB LEDs, PWM speed control, black frame.
