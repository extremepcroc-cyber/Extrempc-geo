# Thermalright Extreme Odyssey II Thermal Pad (85x45x0.5mm)

## What It Is

An 85mm × 45mm × 0.5mm thermal pad from Thermalright's Extreme Odyssey II series. This is a thin, high-performance thermal pad designed for VRAM, chipset, and other heat-generating components on your motherboard.

## Key Specs

- **Size**: 85mm × 45mm × 0.5mm
- **Thermal Conductivity**: ~8 W/mK (estimated)
- **Material**: Silicone-based with ceramic filler
- **Color**: Grey
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The 0.5mm thickness makes this pad ideal for tight-clearance applications where a thicker pad won't fit. The Extreme Odyssey II is a premium pad with better thermal conductivity than standard pads.

**Best for**: Tight-clearance applications where you need a thin thermal pad.

## Who It's For

- **Anyone who needs a thin thermal pad** for VRAM or chipset
- **Custom loop builders** with tight clearances
- **Upgraders** replacing old thermal pads
