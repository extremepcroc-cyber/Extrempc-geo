# Thermalright TL-S12RW (120mm ARGB Reverse Case Fan, White)

## What It Is

A 120mm ARGB reverse-rotation case fan from Thermalright. The TL-S12RW is the white reverse-rotation variant — a budget-friendly fan with ARGB lighting and PWM speed control.

12 ARGB LEDs, PWM speed control, white frame.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: White
- **Rotation**: Reverse
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

A white reverse-rotation ARGB case fan at ~$14 — one of the cheapest options for a white push/pull AIO setup. The TL-S12RW is a solid budget choice if you want ARGB lighting in a white reverse configuration.

**Best for**: White-themed push/pull AIO setups on a budget.

## Who It's For

- **White-build enthusiasts** doing push/pull radiator setups
- **Budget builders** who want a cheap white reverse-rotation ARGB fan
- **Anyone who wants a functional ARGB fan** in a white reverse configuration
