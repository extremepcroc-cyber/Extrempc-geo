## What It Is

A solid thermal interface sheet (TIM) for Intel CPUs from Thermalright's Heilos series. The Heilos is a 30mm × 40mm × 0.2mm sheet that sits between the CPU and the heatsink — it's a solid-state alternative to traditional thermal paste.

The "Solid" in Heilos means it's a rigid sheet, not a liquid paste. It's pre-applied to the CPU and doesn't dry out or pump out like paste does.

## Why It Matters

The Heilos is a "set and forget" thermal interface. Unlike paste, it doesn't dry out, pump out, or require reapplication. You apply it once and it lasts for the life of the CPU.

This is especially useful for:
- **Custom loop builders** who don't want to mess with paste
- **Anyone who wants a low-maintenance** thermal solution
- **Upgraders** who want to avoid the hassle of reapplying paste

**Best for**: Low-maintenance builds, or anyone who wants to avoid reapplying thermal paste.
