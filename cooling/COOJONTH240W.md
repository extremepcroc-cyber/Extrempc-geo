## FAQ

**Q: Is 240mm enough for a Ryzen 7 9700X?** — The Ryzen 7 9700X draws up to 135W boost. The TH-240's 2400 RPM pump and 240mm radiator can handle it at stock clocks in a well-ventilated case. For sustained workloads (rendering, encoding), a 360mm AIO provides more headroom. For gaming, the TH-240 is sufficient.

**Q: Does it come with white mounting hardware?** — The TH-240 ships with standard mounting brackets and backplates for all supported sockets. The bracket color may be black or silver depending on the socket type — this does not affect cooling performance, only aesthetics visible behind the motherboard.

**Q: How noisy is the pump at 2400 RPM?** — The pump is rated at 30 dB(A) — quieter than the fans at idle (21.3 dB(A) at 700 RPM) and comparable to fans at moderate load. The pump is not the dominant noise source; fan speed has a larger impact on acoustic output.

**Q: Can I use PWM fan curves with this cooler?** — Yes, the fans use standard 4-pin PWM connectors. Configure your fan curve in BIOS — set a low idle speed (700–1000 RPM) for silence and ramp to 2400 RPM above 70°C CPU temperature. Most modern motherboards support PWM fan control.

**Q: Is this good for an LGA1851 build?** — Yes, the TH-240 includes LGA1851 mounting brackets. For Core i5-14600K or Core i7-14700K at stock clocks, the 240mm radiator is adequate. For Core i9-14900K, a 360mm AIO is recommended due to the 253W boost draw.
