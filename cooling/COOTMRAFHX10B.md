# Thermalright ARGB & Fan Hub X10 IR (Black)

## What It Is

A 10-port ARGB and fan hub with IR (infrared) remote control from Thermalright. The X10 IR is an upgrade over the basic 10-Port ARGB Hub — it adds a physical hub with an IR remote so you can control your fans' lighting and speed without opening your case or using software.

10 ARGB ports, 10 fan speed control ports, and an IR remote. A practical solution for multi-fan builds.

## Key Specs

- **ARGB Ports**: 10× 3-pin 5V ARGB
- **Fan Speed Ports**: 10× 4-pin PWM fan headers
- **Input**: 1× 3-pin 5V ARGB (motherboard), 1× 4-pin PWM (motherboard)
- **Remote**: IR (infrared) — control lighting and speed without software
- **Color**: Black
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The X10 IR is one of the few ARGB hubs that comes with a physical IR remote. This means you can change your fans' lighting modes and speed on the fly without opening your case or fiddling with software. It's a practical, user-friendly solution for multi-fan builds.

**Best for**: Multi-fan builds where you want to control lighting and speed without software.

## Who It's For

- **Multi-fan ARGB builders** who want a physical remote
- **Anyone who wants to control fans** without opening the case
- **ARGB enthusiasts** with 4–10 fans
