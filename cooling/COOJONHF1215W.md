## What It Is

A 120mm × 15mm ultra-slim ARGB case fan from Jonsbo. At just 15mm thick, this is one of the thinnest 120mm fans you can buy — designed for cases where standard 25mm fans won't fit, like certain compact mATX cases or radiator setups with tight clearance. It has 12 ARGB LEDs and a PWM connector for speed control.

## Why It Matters

The HF1215 is a niche product. Most 120mm case fans are 25mm thick, but some cases (especially compact mATX cases like the Jonsbo D31 or certain ITX cases) only have 15mm of clearance for a fan. The HF1215 is specifically designed for those tight spaces.

The trade-off is airflow — a 15mm fan can't move as much air as a 25mm fan. But if you're building a compact case and need a fan that fits, the HF1215 is one of the only options that does the job with ARGB lighting.

**Best for**: Compact mATX/ITX builds where standard 25mm fans won't fit.
