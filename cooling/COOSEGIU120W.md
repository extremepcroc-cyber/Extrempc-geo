# Segotep Irisflo U120 (120mm ARGB Case Fan, White)

## What It Is

The white variant of the Segotep Irisflo U120. Same as the U120 (black) but with a white frame. 12 ARGB LEDs, PWM speed control.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: White
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

A white ARGB case fan at ~$15 — one of the cheapest options for a white-themed build. The Irisflo U120W is a solid budget choice if you want ARGB lighting in a white build without paying $30+ per fan.

**Best for**: White-build enthusiasts who want cheap ARGB case fans.

## Who It's For

- **White-build enthusiasts**
- **Budget builders** who want case fans with ARGB
- **Anyone who wants a cheap, functional white ARGB fan**
