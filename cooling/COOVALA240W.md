## Selling Points

The Valkyrie A240 ARGB White is an entry-tier 240mm AIO in white — a colorway that typically carries a premium across the AIO market. Valkyrie is a Chinese enthusiast cooling brand known for aggressive pricing and distinctive aesthetics, and the A240 brings that brand's pump technology into the budget segment. Dual 120mm ARGB PWM fans with standard PWM control cover the 500-1800 RPM range, providing quiet idle and responsive load curves.

The A-series is Valkyrie's budget line — below the Dragonfang mid-tier and the V-series LCD flagship. At this entry tier, the A240 shares Valkyrie's pump design language but uses standard PWM fans rather than the magnetic levitation bearings found on the Dragonfang and V360 LCD. The 2800 RPM pump handles 250W TDP — adequate for Ryzen 5 7600X through Ryzen 7 9700X at stock clocks.

White AIO builds are popular in NZ showcase builds — all-white tubing and fan frames contrast well against dark motherboards and cases. The 240mm form factor is the most versatile radiator size for mid-tower builds. Front-mounting a 240mm AIO leaves the top position open for a case exhaust fan, improving internal airflow. In Auckland summer conditions (25°C+ ambient), the combination of front-intake AIO and top exhaust is more effective than a top-mounted AIO alone.
