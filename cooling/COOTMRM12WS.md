# Thermalright TL-M12W-S (120mm Case Fan, White, Bulk)

## What It Is

A 120mm case fan from Thermalright's TL-M series. The TL-M12W-S is a budget-friendly, non-ARGB fan with PWM speed control and a white frame. It's a functional fan that moves air — no RGB, no frills.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: None (non-ARGB)
- **Frame**: White
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The TL-M12W-S is one of the cheapest 120mm case fans on the NZ market at ~$15. It's a white non-ARGB fan — if you're building a white-themed case and just want a functional fan without RGB, it's a solid choice.

**Best for**: White-build enthusiasts who want a clean look without RGB.

## Who It's For
