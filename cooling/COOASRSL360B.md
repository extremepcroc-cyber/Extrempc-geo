## FAQ

**Q: Does Polychrome SYNC work with non-ASRock motherboards?** — Polychrome SYNC software can control the Steel Legend's ARGB lighting independently of the motherboard brand. For deeper integration (syncing with motherboard-attached RGB devices), ASRock motherboards provide the tightest integration, but the AIO functions with any ARGB-compatible board via 5V 3-pin headers.

**Q: 38.3 dB(A) max noise — is that loud for a bedroom setup?** — 38.3 dB(A) is the maximum fan noise at 2500 RPM full load. Under typical gaming loads, fans run at 1200–1800 RPM, producing roughly 25–30 dB(A). At desktop idle, the fans drop further. For bedroom use, configure a conservative fan curve in BIOS to prioritize silence over aggressive cooling.

**Q: Is the USB Type-C connector on the pump head easy to route?** — The pump head uses a USB Type-C connector that terminates in a standard 9-pin internal USB header on the motherboard. The cable is pre-attached and long enough for standard ATX and E-ATX routing. The Type-C latch provides a secure connection that resists accidental disconnection.

**Q: Can I overclock my Ryzen 9 9950X with this cooler?** — The 3100 RPM pump and 360mm radiator handle the 9950X at stock clocks (170W boost) comfortably. Mild overclocking (PBO +50W) is feasible. Aggressive manual overclocks pushing 300W+ sustained benefit from a higher-TDP-rated AIO like the Thermalright Rainbow Vision 360 Turbo.

**Q: What's the difference between the Dark and White versions?** — Identical cooling specs, pump speed, fan speed, and warranty. The Dark version has black fans and black pump housing; the White version (COOASRSL360W) has white fans and white pump housing. Both support the same ARGB lighting effects.
