## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between Dragonfang and A-series?** — The Dragonfang uses magnetic levitation fans rated for longer service life, while the A-series uses standard PWM fans. The Dragonfang also has higher pump RPM (3200 vs 2800). The A-series is budget; the Dragonfang is mid-premium.

**Q: What's the difference between Dragonfang and V-series?** — The V-series (V360 LCD) adds an LCD display on the pump head, higher pump RPM (3400 vs 3200), and custom content support. The Dragonfang has no display but shares maglev fan technology. Choose V-series for display, Dragonfang for value.

**Q: How does maglev bearing compare to FDB?** — Magnetic levitation bearings have less physical contact than FDB (Fluid Dynamic Bearing), resulting in lower friction and longer service life. Both outperform hydraulic and sleeve bearings. Maglev is the higher-end option, found on premium AIOs.

**Q: Does it support LGA1851?** — Yes, the Dragonfang 360 includes LGA1851 mounting brackets. Valkyrie ships with backplates for all current Intel and AMD sockets.

**Q: Is the white version available?** — Yes, the Dragonfang 360 RGB White (COOVALD360W) is available at $259 — a $10 premium for the white colorway, standard across Valkyrie's AIO lineup.
