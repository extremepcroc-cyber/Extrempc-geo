# Thermalright Assassin X 120 Refined SE (White ARGB CPU Air Cooler)

## What It Is

The white variant of the Thermalright Assassin X 120 Refined SE. Same as the AX120 R SE ARGB (black) but with a white heatsink and white ARGB fan.

## Key Specs

- **Type**: Single-tower CPU air cooler
- **Heatpipes**: 6× 6mm (direct contact)
- **Fan**: 1× 120mm ARGB (PWM)
- **Height**: ~155mm (with fan)
- **Socket Support**: AMD AM4/AM5, Intel 115x/1200/1700
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB (on fan)
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Color**: White
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

A white single-tower CPU cooler with ARGB — a rare combination. Most CPU coolers come in black, so if you're building a white-themed build and want a good CPU cooler, the AX120 R SE White is one of the only options.

**Best for**: White-themed builds where you want a good CPU cooler with ARGB.
