# Segotep Irisflo U120F (120mm ARGB Reverse Case Fan, Black)

## What It Is

The reverse-rotation variant of the Segotep Irisflo U120. Same as the U120 but the fan spins in the opposite direction — useful for push/pull radiator setups where you need one fan pushing air in and one pulling air out.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: Black
- **Rotation**: Reverse
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The reverse-rotation fan is the counterpart to the standard (positive) U120. In a push/pull AIO setup, you'd want one standard and one reverse so both fans push air through the radiator in the same direction.

At ~$14, it's one of the cheapest reverse-rotation ARGB fans on the market.

**Best for**: Push/pull AIO setups on a budget.

## Who It's For

- **AIO builders** doing push/pull radiator setups
- **Budget builders** who want a cheap reverse-rotation ARGB fan
- **Anyone who wants a functional ARGB fan** in reverse configuration
