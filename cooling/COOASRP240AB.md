## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit my case?** — 240mm radiators mount in most mid-tower cases at the front or top. Check your case's radiator clearance spec — typically listed as "240mm radiator support" in the case product page. M-ATX cases like Segotep Radiant support 240mm top-mount.

**Q: Is the ARGB compatible with my motherboard?** — The 5V 3-pin ARGB connector is the standard addressable format. Most AM5 and LGA1700 motherboards include at least one 5V ARGB header. If your board only has 12V RGB, the fans will still spin but ARGB lighting won't sync.

**Q: How noisy is the pump?** — The pump runs at approximately 25 dBA, comparable to a quiet office environment. At idle (500 RPM fan speed), the combined fan + pump noise is minimal. Under full load (1800 RPM), expect audible fan noise typical of any 240mm AIO under sustained load.

**Q: Does it come with mounting hardware for both Intel and AMD?** — Yes, the ASRock Pro 240 includes brackets and backplates for Intel LGA1851/LGA1700/LGA1200/LGA115x and AMD AM5/AM4. Thermal paste is pre-applied on the cold plate.

**Q: What's the warranty process if the pump fails?** — ASRock provides 2-year warranty through ExtremePC NZ. Contact ExtremePC's Auckland support — they handle RMA locally without requiring overseas return shipping.
