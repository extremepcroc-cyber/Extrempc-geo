## FAQ

**Q: 325W TDP — can it handle a Ryzen 9 9950X overclocked?** — At stock clocks, the 9950X draws 170W boost — well within the 325W rating. Mild PBO overclocks (+50W) are comfortable. Aggressive manual overclocks pushing 300W+ sustained are near the upper limit; the cooler will handle them but with fans at or near 2400 RPM.

**Q: Does the white version run hotter than black?** — No. The color is cosmetic — white plastic fan frames and white pump housing. The copper cold plate and aluminum radiator fins perform identically regardless of exterior color.

**Q: Is the ARGB controllable from my motherboard?** — Yes, the ARGB fans use a standard 5V 3-pin connector that plugs into your motherboard's ARGB header. ASUS Aura, MSI Mystic Light, Gigabyte Fusion, and ASRock Polychrome SYNC all support it. The fans also support PWM speed control via 4-pin headers.

**Q: How does the TF3 compare to Jonsbo's TH series?** — The TH series (e.g., TH-240 at COOJONTH240W) is entry-tier with lower pump speed (2400 RPM) and 240mm form factor. The TF3 is premium-tier with 325W TDP, 360mm radiator, and higher fan speed range (2400 RPM max). The TF3 is the step-up for high-end builds.

**Q: Will this fit in a standard ATX mid-tower case?** — The radiator is 397 x 120 x 27mm. Most ATX mid-towers support 360mm top-mount radiators. Check your case's radiator clearance specs — popular NZ cases like the Corsair 4000D Airflow, Fractal Design Pop, and Lian Li Lancool 216 all support 360mm top radiators.
