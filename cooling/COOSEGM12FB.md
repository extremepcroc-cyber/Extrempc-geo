# Segotep Meta 12F (120mm Reverse Case Fan, Black)

## What It Is

The reverse-rotation variant of the Segotep Meta 12. Same as the Meta 12 but the fan spins in the opposite direction — useful for push/pull radiator setups.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: None (non-ARGB)
- **Frame**: Black
- **Rotation**: Reverse
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The reverse-rotation fan is the counterpart to the standard (positive) Meta 12. In a push/pull AIO setup, you'd want one standard and one reverse so both fans push air through the radiator in the same direction.

**Best for**: Push/pull AIO setups on a budget.

## Who It's For

- **AIO builders** doing push/pull radiator setups
- **Budget builders** who want a cheap reverse-rotation fan
- **Anyone who wants a functional fan** in reverse configuration
