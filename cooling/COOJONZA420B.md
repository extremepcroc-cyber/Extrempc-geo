## What It Is

A 420mm (14-inch) ARGB case fan from Jonsbo — one of the largest single 120mm-compatible fans you can buy. The "420" refers to the fan diameter: 420mm ≈ 14 inches. It's designed for cases with 140mm or 160mm fan mounts, or for use as a radiator fan on 420mm radiators (some 420mm radiators accept 140mm fans).

The fan has 12 ARGB LEDs, a PWM connector for speed control, and a black frame. It's a big, loud, high-airflow fan — not a quiet one.

## Why It Matters

420mm fans are a niche product. Most cases use 120mm or 140mm fans, but some high-end cases and radiators support 160mm or even larger fans. The ZA-420 is designed for those specific use cases.

The main advantage of a 420mm fan over three 140mm fans is that it moves a lot of air in a single unit. The downside is that it's loud at high RPMs and not compatible with most standard case mounts.

**Best for**: High-end custom loop builders who need a large fan for a 420mm radiator or a case with 160mm+ fan mounts.
