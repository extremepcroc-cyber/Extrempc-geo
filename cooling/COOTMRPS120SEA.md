# Thermalright Phantom Spirit 120 SE (ARGB CPU Air Cooler)

## What It Is

A dual-tower CPU air cooler with 2 ARGB fans from Thermalright. The Phantom Spirit 120 SE is part of Thermalright's Phantom Spirit series — a high-performance air cooler with a bit more flair than the PA120.

The "SE" variant adds 2 120mm ARGB fans for some RGB in your build. The cooler itself is a dual-tower design with 6 heatpipes.

## Key Specs

- **Type**: Dual-tower CPU air cooler
- **Heatpipes**: 6× 6mm (direct contact)
- **Fans**: 2× 120mm ARGB (PWM)
- **Height**: ~155mm (with fans)
- **Socket Support**: AMD AM4/AM5, Intel 115x/1200/1700/1851
- **LEDs**: 24 ARGB (12 per fan)
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The Phantom Spirit 120 SE is a high-performance CPU air cooler with ARGB — a good choice for builds where you want good cooling and a bit of RGB. It's slightly cheaper than the PA120 White ARGB, making it a solid budget option for a high-performance cooler with RGB.

**Best for**: High-end builds where you want good cooling and ARGB.

## Who It's For

- **High-end builders** who want a good CPU cooler with ARGB
- **Overclockers** who want a bit of RGB in their build
- **Anyone who wants a dual-tower cooler** with ARGB at a reasonable price
