## What It Is

A dual-tower CPU air cooler with 2 ARGB fans from Thermalright. The Phantom Spirit 120 SE is part of Thermalright's Phantom Spirit series — a high-performance air cooler with a bit more flair than the PA120.

The "SE" variant adds 2 120mm ARGB fans for some RGB in your build. The cooler itself is a dual-tower design with 6 heatpipes.

- **Type**: Dual-tower CPU air cooler

- **Socket Support**: AMD AM4/AM5, Intel 115x/1200/1700/1851
