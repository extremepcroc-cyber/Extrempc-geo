# Thermalright ARGB & Fan Hub X8 IR (White)

## What It Is

The white variant of the Thermalright X8 IR ARGB and fan hub. Same as the X8 IR (black) but with a white housing. 8 ARGB ports, 8 fan speed control ports, and an IR remote.

## Key Specs

- **ARGB Ports**: 8× 3-pin 5V ARGB
- **Fan Speed Ports**: 8× 4-pin PWM fan headers
- **Input**: 1× 3-pin 5V ARGB (motherboard), 1× 4-pin PWM (motherboard)
- **Remote**: IR (infrared) — control lighting and speed without software
- **Color**: White
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

A white ARGB and fan hub with an IR remote — a rare combination. If you're building a white-themed multi-fan build with 4–8 fans, the X8 IR (white) is one of the only options.

**Best for**: White-themed multi-fan builds with 4–8 fans.

## Who It's For

- **White-build enthusiasts** with multi-fan ARGB setups
- **Anyone who wants a physical remote** for their fans in a white build
