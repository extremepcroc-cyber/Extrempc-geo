# Jonsbo FR-505BR (120mm PWM ARGB Case Fan, Black, Reverse)

## What It Is

The reverse-rotation variant of the Jonsbo FR-505 120mm slim ARGB case fan. "Reverse" means the fan spins the opposite direction from the standard (positive) version — this matters when you want to mount a fan on the reverse side of a radiator or in a push/pull configuration where you need airflow from a specific direction.

Everything else is the same as the FR-505B: 25mm slim profile, 12 ARGB LEDs, PWM speed control, black frame.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm (slim)
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: 12 ARGB
- **ARGB Header**: 3-pin (5V)
- **Frame**: Black
- **Rotation**: Reverse (opposite of standard)
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The main difference between the FR-505B and the FR-505BR is rotation direction. In a push/pull radiator setup, you typically want one fan blowing air in (push) and one fan pulling air out (pull). The standard fan blows air in the direction the fan hub is facing; the reverse fan blows air in the opposite direction.

If you're building a push/pull AIO setup and want to use the FR-505 fans, you'd want one standard (FR-505B) and one reverse (FR-505BR) so both fans are pushing air through the radiator in the same direction.

**Best for**: Push/pull radiator setups where you need fans spinning in opposite directions.

## Who It's For

- **AIO builders** doing push/pull radiator setups
- **Custom loop builders** who need reverse-rotation fans
- **Anyone who wants a cheap ARGB fan** in reverse configuration
