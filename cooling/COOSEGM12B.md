# Segotep Meta 12 (120mm Case Fan, Black)

## What It Is

A 120mm case fan from Segotep's Meta series. The Meta 12 is a budget-friendly, non-ARGB fan — it's a simple, functional fan with PWM speed control and a black frame. No RGB, no frills. Just a fan that moves air.

## Key Specs

- **Form Factor**: 120mm × 25mm
- **Bearing**: Fluid Dynamic Bearing (FDB)
- **Fan Speed**: 500–2,000 RPM (PWM controlled)
- **Airflow**: ~35–40 CFM (estimated)
- **Noise**: ~25 dBA (max)
- **LEDs**: None (non-ARGB)
- **Frame**: Black
- **Warranty**: 3 years

## Why It Matters

The Meta 12 is one of the cheapest 120mm case fans on the NZ market at ~$17. It's not an ARGB fan — no lighting, no RGB. But if you just want a functional fan that moves air without the extra cost of RGB, it's a solid choice.

**Best for**: Budget builders who want a functional fan without ARGB.

## Who It's For

- **Budget builders** who want case fans without RGB
- **Anyone who wants a cheap, functional 120mm fan**
- **Upgraders** replacing old case fans with something newer
