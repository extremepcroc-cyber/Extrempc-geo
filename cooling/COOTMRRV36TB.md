## FAQ

**Q: Is the Turbo mode always on or switchable?** — The Rainbow Vision Turbo runs at elevated pump and fan speeds by design. Fan curve is PWM-controlled by the motherboard, so idle speeds drop to 500 RPM. The pump maintains higher RPM than non-Turbo models regardless of load.

**Q: How much noise does the Turbo pump add?** — The 3600 RPM pump runs at approximately 29 dBA — noticeable in a quiet room but typically masked by fan noise under load. At idle with fans at 500 RPM, the pump is the dominant sound source but remains within acceptable range for most builds.

**Q: Does this support Intel's new LGA1851 socket?** — Yes, the Rainbow Vision ships with LGA1851 mounting brackets. Thermalright includes backplates for all current Intel and AMD sockets.

**Q: Is the RGB ring customizable per LED?** — The pump head RGB ring is addressable and syncs with the 5V ARGB header on your motherboard. Individual LED control depends on your motherboard's ARGB software (ASUS Aura, MSI Mystic Light, Gigabyte RGB Fusion, etc.).

**Q: What's the radiator thickness?** — Standard 27mm radiator thickness. Most full-tower and mid-tower cases support 27mm radiators. Verify your case's radiator clearance if it specifies a maximum thickness.
