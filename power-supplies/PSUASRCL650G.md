## FAQ

- **Q: Is 650W enough for my build?**
  A: For a Ryzen 5 / Core i5 + a mid-range GPU (RTX 4070 / RX 7800 XT class), yes. For higher-end GPUs (RTX 4070 Ti Super / RX 7900 XTX), step up to 750W or 850W.

- **Q: Non-modular — will I have leftover cables?**
  A: Yes, non-modular means all cables are fixed, so you'll have some unused ones. In a clean ATX case, tuck them away. For full cable management, a modular PSU is the pick.

- **Q: ATX 3.1 — what's the benefit?**
  A: The newer 12V-2x6 GPU connector and updated transient response. An ATX 3.1 PSU is ready for current-gen and next-gen GPUs.
