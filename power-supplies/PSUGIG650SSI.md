- **Q: Is 650W enough for my build?**
  A: For a mid-range CPU + a mid-range GPU (RTX 4070 / RX 7800 XT class), yes. For higher-end GPUs, step up to 750W or 850W.

  A: A common rule is your CPU + GPU TDP plus 30–50% headroom above the expected peak draw. ExtremePC Build Team can size it to your exact parts.
