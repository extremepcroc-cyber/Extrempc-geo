## FAQ

**Q: Does the 3.5mm connection work on PS5 and Xbox?** — Yes. The 3.5mm analog connection is the standard console headset interface. The detachable boom mic works for voice chat on all three major consoles.

**Q: Is the audio good for competitive FPS?** — The 50mm TriForce driver delivers a solid mid-range and bass response. It is not a DTS-certified spatial audio headset, so for critical positional audio in CS2 or Valorant, a DTS-certified option like the HyperX Cloud Stinger 2 is the stronger choice.

**Q: Is the white colourway durable?** — Razer uses a light-grey fabric cover on the white variant rather than pure white plastic, which resists staining better than glossy white surfaces. Normal wear over 2+ years will show some fading on the headband.

**Q: How comfortable is it for long sessions?** — The memory foam ear cushions and leatherette cover are comfortable for 3-4 hour sessions. For 6+ hour sessions, the slightly heavier clamping force may cause pressure on the top of the head.
