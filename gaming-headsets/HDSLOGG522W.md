## FAQ

**Q: How does LIGHTSPEED compare to Bluetooth latency?** — LIGHTSPEED operates on a dedicated 2.4GHz channel with sub-millisecond report intervals. Bluetooth audio has a typical latency of 100-200ms. For competitive FPS, LIGHTSPEED is the clear choice.

**Q: Does the 7.1 virtual surround work without G HUB?** — No. The 7.1 virtual surround is implemented through Logitech G HUB software. Without G HUB installed, the headset operates in standard stereo mode.

**Q: Is the Blue VO!CE mic good for streaming?** — The Blue VO!CE boom mic with AI noise reduction is adequate for Discord and team comms. For serious streaming, pair with a dedicated USB microphone. The Blue VO!CE excels at rejecting keyboard noise.

**Q: What is the real-world battery life?** — Logitech rates 40 hours. Real-world usage with RGB off is approximately 32-36 hours. At 5 hours of daily gaming, expect to charge once every 6-7 days.
