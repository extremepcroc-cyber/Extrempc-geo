## FAQ

**Q: Can I use HyperSpeed and Bluetooth at the same time?** — Yes. The Kraken V4 Wireless supports simultaneous HyperSpeed and Bluetooth connections. Game audio routes through HyperSpeed while phone calls and voice chat route through Bluetooth.

**Q: How does THX Spatial Audio compare to DTS Headphone:X?** — Both are certified virtual surround technologies aimed at positional audio in games. Which one sounds better is a matter of personal preference, so read reviews of the specific headsets you are considering.

**Q: Does Chroma RGB work without Razer Synapse?** — Chroma RGB effects are limited without Razer Synapse installed. Basic static colours work, but the full Chroma ecosystem (game-reactive lighting, sync with other Razer devices) requires the Synapse software.

**Q: What is the battery life?** — Razer rates 70 hours on HyperSpeed.
