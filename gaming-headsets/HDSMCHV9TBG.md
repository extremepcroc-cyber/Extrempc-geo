## FAQ

**Q: How does the 60mm driver compare to the 53mm in the V9 Pro?** — The 60mm driver is larger than the V9 Pro's 53mm driver. For general gaming and media, driver size alone does not decide sound quality.

**Q: How does the magnetic charging station work?** — The headset docks magnetically on the stand. The stand provides a USB-C connection for power. There is no cable to manage — the headset snaps onto the stand and charges.

**Q: Is the black-gold colourway durable?** — MCHOSE uses a matte-finish plastic in the black-gold colourway, which resists fingerprints and minor scuffs better than glossy finishes.
