## FAQ

**Q: Is DTS Headphone:X the same as virtual surround software?** — No. DTS Headphone:X is a licensed spatial audio engine that processes multi-channel audio through stereo drivers with measured, consistent results. Generic "virtual surround" software (Windows Sonic, Discord noise gate) is not the same technology. DTS Headphone:X is what makes footstep directionality reliable in competitive titles.

**Q: Does the 2.4GHz dongle work with PS5 or Xbox?** — No. The USB-A 2.4GHz dongle is designed for PC (Windows). For console use, the 3.5mm audio jack provides wired fallback with mono mic.

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?** — HyperX rates it at 45 hours. Real-world usage with DTS Headphone:X enabled and the RGB off is approximately 35-40 hours. At 5 hours of daily gaming, expect to charge once every 7-8 days.

**Q: Is the mic good enough for Discord or team comms?** — The detachable boom mic with noise cancellation is adequate for team voice chat. It is not a studio microphone — expect some room noise in echoey rooms. For serious streaming, pair with a dedicated USB microphone.
