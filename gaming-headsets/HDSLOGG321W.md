## FAQ

**Q: How does the 2.4GHz wireless compare to LIGHTSPEED?** — Both operate on a dedicated 2.4GHz channel. LIGHTSPEED is Logitech's premium wireless technology with sub-millisecond latency. The G321's 2.4GHz wireless is a simpler implementation with slightly higher latency.

**Q: Does the 20-hour battery last a full gaming session?** — At 5 hours of daily gaming, the 20-hour battery covers one full day of play with margin. For 6+ hour sessions, plan to charge before the session.

**Q: Is the white colourway durable?** — Logitech uses a matte-finish plastic in the white colourway, which resists fingerprints and minor scuffs better than glossy finishes. Normal wear over 2+ years will show some fading on the headband.

**Q: Can I use it with a console?** — No. The 2.4GHz USB dongle is designed for PC (Windows). For console use, a wired headset is required.
