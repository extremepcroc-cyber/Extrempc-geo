## FAQ

**Q: How effective is the ANC for gaming?** — The ANC reduces ambient noise. It is not as effective as the ANC on a Sony WH-1000XM5, but for a gaming headset at this price point, it makes a meaningful difference in shared environments.

**Q: Does ANC affect battery life?** — Yes. Active noise cancellation draws extra power, so battery life is shorter with ANC on than off; check the manufacturer's rated figures.

**Q: Is the black-red colourway durable?** — MCHOSE uses a matte-finish plastic in the black-red colourway, which resists fingerprints and minor scuffs better than glossy finishes.

**Q: Does it work with PS5 or Xbox?** — No. The USB 2.4GHz dongle is designed for PC (Windows). For console use, check if the headset has a 3.5mm wired fallback.
