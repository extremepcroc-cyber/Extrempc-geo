## FAQ

**Q: What is the difference between the Core and the Stinger 2 Wireless?** — Both carry DTS Headphone:X and 45-hour battery life. The Stinger 2 uses a slightly more premium headband construction and ear cushion material. The Core is the budget entry point to the same DTS technology at a lower price.

**Q: Does DTS Headphone:X work on all platforms?** — DTS Headphone:X is a PC (Windows) technology. It requires the DTS Sound Unbound or DTS Headphone:X software to be installed. On consoles, the headset falls back to standard stereo audio.

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?** — HyperX rates it at 45 hours. Real-world usage with DTS Headphone:X enabled is approximately 35-40 hours. At 5 hours of daily gaming, expect to charge once every 7-8 days.

**Q: Is the white colourway durable?** — HyperX uses a light-grey fabric cover on the white variant rather than pure white plastic, which resists staining better than glossy white surfaces. Normal wear over 2+ years will show some fading on the headband.
