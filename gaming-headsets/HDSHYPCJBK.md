## FAQ

**Q: What is the difference between the Cloud Jet and the Stinger 2?** — Both use 2.4GHz dedicated wireless. The Stinger 2 carries DTS Headphone:X certification, which the Cloud Jet does not. The Stinger 2 also uses a 40mm driver vs the Cloud Jet's 45mm. For certified spatial audio, the Stinger 2 is the stronger choice.

**Q: How does the 45mm driver compare to the Stinger 2's 40mm?** — The 45mm driver in the Cloud Jet delivers a slightly wider soundstage with more bass. The 40mm driver in the Stinger 2 is tuned for a more neutral response with DTS Headphone:X processing. Both deliver solid sound for their price tier.

**Q: Does the 2.4GHz dongle work with PS5 or Xbox?** — No. The USB 2.4GHz dongle is designed for PC (Windows). For console use, check if the headset has a 3.5mm wired fallback.

**Q: What is the real-world battery life?** — HyperX rates it at 42 hours. Real-world usage is approximately 35-40 hours. At 5 hours of daily gaming, expect to charge once every 7-8 days.
