## FAQ

**Q: Does Chroma RGB work without Razer Synapse?** — Chroma RGB effects are limited without Razer Synapse installed. Basic static colours work, but the full Chroma ecosystem (game-reactive lighting, sync with other Razer devices) requires the Synapse software.

**Q: Is the fixed mic a problem?** — The fixed unidirectional boom mic works well for team comms. The limitation is that you cannot remove it for movie or music listening. If you regularly switch between gaming and media, the detachable mic on the BlackShark v2 X is more flexible.

**Q: Does it support PS5 and Xbox?** — Yes. The 3.5mm analog connection is the standard console headset interface. The Chroma RGB will not be visible on consoles, but the audio and mic function normally.

**Q: How does the audio compare to the BlackShark v2 X?** — Both use the 50mm TriForce titanium-coated driver. The Kraken V4 X is in a higher tier of Razer's headset line, which reflects the Chroma RGB and slightly different build materials rather than a driver upgrade.
