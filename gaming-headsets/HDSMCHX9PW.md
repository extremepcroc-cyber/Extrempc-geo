## FAQ

**Q: Does the 2.4GHz dongle work with PS5 or Xbox?** — No. The USB 2.4GHz dongle is designed for PC (Windows). For console use, check if the headset has a 3.5mm wired fallback — if not, it is PC-only.

**Q: How does the audio compare to a Razer or HyperX headset?** — The 40mm drivers are tuned for a balanced response with mid-range emphasis. They are not DTS-certified, so for critical positional audio in competitive FPS, a DTS-certified option like the HyperX Cloud Stinger 2 is the stronger choice. For general gaming and media at value pricing, the X9 Pro delivers solid sound.

**Q: What is the real-world battery life?** — MCHOSE rates it at 40 hours. Real-world usage with the RGB off is approximately 30-35 hours. At 5 hours of daily gaming, expect to charge once every 6-7 days.

**Q: Is the 1-year warranty sufficient?** — The 1-year MCHOSE warranty is shorter than Razer's 2-year or HyperX's 2-year warranty. For a headset in the value tier, 1 year is the standard warranty period. If you are concerned about long-term warranty coverage, the Razer or HyperX options provide an additional year.
