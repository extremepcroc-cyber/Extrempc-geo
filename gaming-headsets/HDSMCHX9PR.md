## FAQ

**Q: Does the 2.4GHz dongle work with PS5 or Xbox?** — No. The USB 2.4GHz dongle is designed for PC (Windows). For console use, check if the headset has a 3.5mm wired fallback.

**Q: How does the audio compare to a Razer or HyperX headset?** — The 40mm drivers are tuned for a balanced response. They are not DTS-certified, so for critical positional audio in competitive FPS, a DTS-certified option like the HyperX Cloud Stinger 2 is the stronger choice.

**Q: What is the real-world battery life?** — MCHOSE rates it at 40 hours. Real-world usage with the RGB off is approximately 30-35 hours. At 5 hours of daily gaming, expect to charge once every 6-7 days.

**Q: Is the rose red colourway durable?** — MCHOSE uses a matte-finish plastic in the rose red colourway, which resists fingerprints and minor scuffs better than glossy finishes. Normal wear over 2+ years will show some fading on the headband.
