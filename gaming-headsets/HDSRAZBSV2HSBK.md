## FAQ

**Q: How does HyperSpeed compare to Bluetooth latency?** — HyperSpeed operates on a dedicated 2.4GHz channel with 1ms report intervals. Bluetooth audio has a typical latency of 100-200ms. For competitive FPS, HyperSpeed is the clear choice.

**Q: Does the 12-hour battery last a full gaming session?** — At 5 hours of daily gaming, the 12-hour battery covers one full day of play with margin. For 6+ hour sessions, plan to charge before the session.

**Q: Is the audio different from the V2 Pro?** — Both use the 50mm TriForce titanium-coated driver. The V2 Pro adds THX Spatial Audio software processing, which the HyperSpeed does not include.

**Q: Can I use it with a console?** — No. The HyperSpeed 2.4GHz dongle is designed for PC (Windows). For console use, a wired headset is required.
