## FAQ

**Q: How does the 2.4GHz wireless compare to LIGHTSPEED?** — Both operate on a dedicated 2.4GHz channel. LIGHTSPEED is Logitech's premium wireless technology with sub-millisecond latency. The G321's 2.4GHz wireless is a simpler implementation with slightly higher latency. For competitive FPS, the G522 with LIGHTSPEED is the stronger choice.

**Q: Does the 20-hour battery last a full gaming session?** — At 5 hours of daily gaming, the 20-hour battery covers one full day of play with margin. For 6+ hour sessions, plan to charge before the session.

**Q: Is the audio good for competitive FPS?** — The 40mm drivers deliver a solid mid-range response. They are not DTS-certified or 7.1 virtual surround capable, so for critical positional audio in CS2 or Valorant, the G522 with 7.1 virtual surround is the stronger choice.

**Q: Can I use it with a console?** — No. The 2.4GHz USB dongle is designed for PC (Windows). For console use, a wired headset is required.
