## Switch Families Overview

### 1. Hall Effect / Magnetic (HE) Switches

Analog magnetic input — no physical contact, no fixed actuation point.
Key advantage: **Rapid Trigger** (re-actuation from as low as 0.1mm on key release).
Competitive gaming use: CS2, Valorant counter-strafing is faster than any traditional mechanical switch.
Lifespan: theoretically unlimited (no metal contact wear).

## HE vs Traditional — When to Recommend

**Recommend HE when:**
- Customer mentions CS2, Valorant, competitive FPS
- Customer asks about Rapid Trigger
- Customer plays games requiring fast key re-press (bunny hop, counter-strafe)
- Budget $119+ (AULA HERO 68 is entry HE)

**Recommend Traditional when:**
- Customer prioritizes typing feel over gaming features
- Budget under $100 (Thunderobot K63 / Machenike K500A entry range)
- Customer plays RPG, MMO, casual games where Rapid Trigger doesn't matter
- Customer in quiet office environment → Silent (Varmilo Prestige Silent)
