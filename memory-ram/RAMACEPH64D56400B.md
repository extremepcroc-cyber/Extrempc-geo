## FAQ

- **Q: 6400 CL32 vs 6000 CL36 — which to pick for AM5?**
  A: 6000 CL36 (Vesta II / PALLAS II) keeps Infinity Fabric in 1:1 sync — the AM5 sweet spot. 6400 CL32 gives higher bandwidth but a slight FCLK desync. For mainstream gaming, 6000 CL36 is the safe pick. For creator workloads or Intel builds, 6400 CL32 is a clear step up.

- **Q: Why not 2× 16GB for 32GB?**
  A: If you only need 32GB, 2× 16GB is cheaper and sufficient. 64GB is for when you genuinely need the capacity (VMs, large 3D scenes, 8K timelines, heavy compilation).

- **Q: Will RGB sync with my non-Predator motherboard?**
  A: Yes — compatible with ASUS Aura Sync, MSI Mystic Light, Gigabyte RGB Fusion, ASRock Polychrome SYNC.

- **Q: Black aesthetic — what builds does it match?**
  A: Dark-themed gaming builds, red/black RGB themes, blue/black RGB. For white / silver themes, look at other Predator SKUs.

- **Q: Will it fit under my CPU air cooler?**
  A: Hermes RGB is ~44mm tall due to the lightbar. For tall air coolers (Noctua NH-D15, Phantom Spirit), check clearance. AIO builds have no issue.
