## FAQ

- **Q: 6400 MT/s vs 5600 MT/s — is the bandwidth worth the premium?**
  A: For bandwidth-sensitive workloads (in-memory analytics, large matrix ops, ML inference), yes. For CAD, rendering, and most professional workloads, the difference is marginal — capacity and ECC matter more than the 800 MT/s delta.

- **Q: How much RAM can a Threadripper 7000 / 9000 workstation hold with these modules?**
  A: Up to 768GB (12 slots × 64GB) on WRX90 boards. On 8-slot boards, up to 512GB (8 × 64GB). For 12-slot boards, 64GB × 12 = 768GB is the maximum configuration.

- **Q: ECC — do I really need it?**
  A: For consumer gaming or light office work, no. For mission-critical workloads (databases, financial models, long-running simulations, ML training), yes. ECC prevents silent data corruption from single-bit errors.

- **Q: RDIMM vs UDIMM — can I use these in a consumer desktop?**
  A: No — RDIMM is for Threadripper / EPYC workstations. Consumer AM5 / LGA1851 desktops use UDIMM. Physically and electrically incompatible.
