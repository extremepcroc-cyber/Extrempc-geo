## FAQ

- **Q: Why 5600 CL46 — isn't that slow?**
  A: 5600 MT/s is the JEDEC baseline DDR5 speed and runs rock-stable on both AM5 and LGA1851 without XMP tuning. It's the safe, reliable choice. For peak gaming bandwidth, a 6000/6400 EXPO/XMP kit is the step up. For 64GB capacity + stability, this is a strong value.

- **Q: 64GB — do I really need that much?**
  A: For mainstream gaming, 32GB is sufficient. 64GB is for creators (VMs, large 3D scenes, 8K timelines, heavy compilation, data science) who regularly exceed 32GB.

- **Q: Works with both AMD and Intel?**
  A: Yes — DDR5-5600 is the JEDEC baseline speed supported by both AM5 (Ryzen 7000/9000) and LGA1851 (Core Ultra 200) platforms.

- **Q: Non-RGB — what builds does it suit?**
  A: Compact / SFF builds, clean-aesthetic builds, and any build where you want maximum cooler clearance. For showcase RGB builds, see Predator Hermes or G.SKILL M5 Neo.
